About Us

History & Legacy

Discover the rich history and enduring legacy of MIT-WPU. With a foundation dating back to 1983, our institution has consistently excelled in delivering quality education and encouraging innovation. Explore our remarkable journey from humble beginnings to becoming an institution known for academics, research contributions, and social impact. Uncover the legacy of MIT-WPU, shaping the future of education for generations to come.

An institution of higher education with academic standards, MIT-WPU’s rich legacy dates back more than 40 years.

Visionaries of Education: The Birth of MIT-WPU

Between 1981 and 1983, MIT was conceived as an inspiration, an institution that stood decades ahead of its time. It was born from the creative zeal to redefine education, embracing innovation. During this pivotal period, the seeds of a remarkable educational journey were sown, laying the foundation for an institution that would continuously inspire and shape the future of education, pushing the boundaries of what was thought possible in academia.

The Inaugural Years of MIT (Now MIT-WPU)

MIT (now MIT-WPU) embarked on its journey with unwavering determination in 1983. At its inception, a dedicated team of professors passionate about education laid the foundation upon which this institution would thrive. The auspicious Bhoomi Pujan, a ceremony, marked the inception of a remarkable voyage. MAEERs welcomed its first batch of Electronics and Telecommunication students, setting the stage for academics. Additionally, establishing a foundation stone for a workshop reinforced the commitment to hands-on learning, ensuring an educational experience for all who walked through its doors. This initial chapter in MIT-WPU history was a testament to its dedication to developing knowledge and innovation.

Global Recognition and Cultural Milestones: The Institutional Journey

From 1996 to 2003, MIT-WPU made remarkable strides toward its goal of becoming a global university. Notably, it established a UNESCO Chair, signifying an unwavering commitment to elevating international education standards. Hosting the World Philosophers Meet created a global platform for intellectual exchange, developing an academic community. The institution's dedication to peace earned it the World Peace Prize, recognizing its impactful peace initiatives. Furthermore, hosting the 69th Akhil Bharatiya Marathi Sahitya Sammelan marked a cultural milestone, highlighting its multifaceted role in education and culture. These accomplishments underscored university’s significance in academia and its global role as a champion of education, peace, and culture.

A Decade of Achievements and Impact

Between 2004 and 2014, MIT-WPU made significant additions to it's never ending list of achievements. The Bharat Asmita National Awards celebrated national pride, acknowledging significant national contributors. Establishing Vishwashanti Gurukul encouraged education, developing knowledge and character. The Silver Jubilee Year marked a quarter-century. The Bharatiya Chhatra Sansad trained student leaders, shaping the nation's future. MIT-WPU hosting of the National Women's Parliament passionately championed gender equality, offering women a powerful platform to advocate for their rights. This decade, we exemplified the institution's commitment to national pride, education, youth leadership, and gender equality, leaving a profound societal impact.

Expanding Horizons: Global Vision and Commitment to Peace

From 2015 to 2019, MIT (now MIT-WPU) embarked on an era of expansion, establishing Avantika University and MIT-Shillong, broadening its educational influence. Notably, the formation of the World Peace Dome in Loni, Pune, symbolised global harmony, reflecting the institution's commitment to peace worldwide. A poignant Public Dedication Ceremony underscored the university's unwavering dedication to peace and education. In this period, our global vision and its role as a beacon of enlightenment and harmony in education became even more pronounced.

MIT-WPU: Leading the Future of Education through Innovation

Since 2021, MIT-WPU has been on a journey as it went through digital transformation, blending modern teaching methods with its rich tradition, showcasing adaptability and progressive thinking. In 2022, the institution earned the "Great Place to Work" title, affirming its in education and innovation. This recognition was a testament to an enduring commitment to education, peace, and innovation. It encapsulated their inspiring journey, marked by growth and a profound positive impact on the world - a legacy that continues to shape the future of higher education in India, setting benchmarks for the world to follow.
