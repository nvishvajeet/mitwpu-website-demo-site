Hostel Facilities

Discover the Best Hostel Facilities at MIT-WPU - Experience comfortable and modern accommodations for students. Explore our well-equipped hostels, study spaces, and a campus community. Amenities are Wifi, Gym, Canteen, Lifts, Air Conditioned rooms, sharing options etc.

Welcome to the MIT World Peace University Hostel!

Located in the city of Pune, Maharashtra, our hostel offers accommodation facilities designed to meet the needs of our students. We prioritize creating a comfortable and safe environment, allowing students to focus on their academic goals without the worry of finding suitable accommodation. Our hostel boasts a capacity of 3000 beds, featuring well-planned and high-quality fittings for a comfortable stay. We understand the importance of a balanced lifestyle, which is why we provide various recreational facilities including a gym, game rooms with carrom, pool, chess, and other board games, study rooms, and a basketball court, ensuring students can relax and unwind after a long day of classes.

At MIT-WPU, we are dedicated to providing a living experience, ensuring the safety and security of our residents while creating a welcoming and student-friendly environment. Our commitment is to support students in achieving their academic aspirations by offering accommodation services tailored to their needs.

Join us at MIT-WPU Hostel and begin a journey toward academic success in a home away from home.

Our Amenities

  • Single, double, and triple occupancy with furniture.
  • RO treated drinking water at convenient locations on every alternate floor of the hostel building.
  • Washing machines, Induction,and microwaves are also available in common area.
  • Daily provision of breakfast and dinner, lunch on weekends with versatile menus
  • Student shuttle facilities from hostel to university campus
  • Vigilant 24x7 security guards
  • Student recreation zones like basic gym, game room, study room, and dance/yoga rooms (Subject to hostel)
  • Hot Water is available in all rooms.
  • Laundry services
  • Premises under CCTV surveillance 24x7
  • The hostel premises are fire compliant and are equipped with fire extinguishers
  • Treatment plants like STP/WTP are installed at all premises.

In-room Description

  • Beds with mattresses and bed sheets
  • Study desks
  • Independent wardrobes for every bed
  • Modern European style attached/common washrooms.
  • 24x7 Wi-Fi access

Girls Hostels

The Girls' Hostel at MIT-WPU is a secure and developing space designed for the comfort and convenience of female students. Located within the areas of Pune, it offers modern amenities, well-appointed rooms, and 24/7 security. More than just a place to stay, it encourages a sense of community, encouraging lifelong friendships and supporting development.

Boys Hostels

The Boys' Hostel at MIT-WPU is a secure residence tailored to male students' needs. Situated within the lively campus, it provides modern amenities, comfortable rooms, and round-the-clock security. More than a living space, the hostel promotes a sense of brotherhood, encouraging strong connections and supporting comprehensive personal development.

FAQs for MIT-WPU Hostel

  • Q. What types of rooms are available at the MIT-WPU Hostel?

    A. The MIT-WPU Hostel offers single, double, and triple occupancy rooms with modern European-style attached or common washrooms.

  • Q: Is the hostel facility available for both boys & girls?

    A: Yes, separate hostels are available for boys and girls with dedicated amenities and security measures.

  • Q: Are meals provided in the hostel?

    A: Yes, daily breakfast and dinner are provided, along with lunch on weekends. The menu is versatile to cater to various preferences.

  • Q: What recreational facilities are available?

    A: The hostel includes a gym, game rooms with carroms, pool table, chess, and dance/yoga rooms. Each hostel has a list of amenities which is mentioned in the hostel manual.

  • Q: Is hot water available?

    A: Hot water is available in all rooms during specified times in the morning and evening.

  • Q: Are laundry facilities provided?

    A: Yes, washing machines and laundry services are also available.

  • Q: Is Wi-Fi available in the hostel?

    A: yes, the hostel provides 24x7 Wi-Fi access.

  • Q: Are there medical facilities on-site?

    A: A doctor visits the hostel once a week.

  • Q: What are the fees for hostel and mess?

    A: Fees vary based on room type AC/ non-AC facilities. Detailed fee structure hostel wise is available in the hostel manual.

  • Q: What is the payment process?

    A: Payments must be made online via a link provided by the hostel administration. Room allotments are confirmed upon the full payment of the first installment.

  • Q: Is there a refundable security deposit?

    A: Yes, a refundable security deposit of ₹ 25,000must be paid along with the first installment.

  • Q: Are parents allowed to visit the hostel?

    A: Parents can visit designated visitor areas but are not allowed beyond specified zones to maintain student privacy.

  • Q: What is the policy for late arrivals?

    A: Hostel gates close at 9.30 PM. Latecomers must sign a register and may face disciplinary action for repeated delays.

  • Q: Can students cook in their room?

    A: Cooking in rooms is not allowed, but students can use the allocated common kitchen areas.

  • Q: What security measures are in place?

    A: The hostel has vigilant 24/7 security guards and CCTV coverage throughout the premises. In addition to that digital attendance tracking mechanism.

  • Q: What are the rules for visitors?

    A: Visitors must register and follow the hostel entry protocols. Male visitors are not allowed in girls hostels and vice versa.

  • Q: Are there any restrictions on using electronic appliances?

    A: Only essential appliances like mobile and laptop chargers are permitted in rooms. Additional gadgets require approval.

  • Additional Information

    Q: Can students take leave during their stay in a hostel during the academic year?

    A: Leave requests must be submitted three days in advance with parental approval.

    Q: How can I contact the hostel administration for inquiries?

    A: you can reach the hostel team at hostel.admin@mitwpu.edu.in or call +91-8605003970.

  • General Information

    Q: Where are the hostels located, and how far are they from the university?

    A: Hostels are located in various areas like Ambegaon, Bavdhan, Karvenagar, and Kothrud. The distance ranges from 2.7 km to 10.5 km. Bus shuttle services are provided from the hostel to college campus.

    Q: Are there both AC & non-AC room options?

    A: Yes, students can choose between AC and non-AC rooms depending on their preference and budget for the respective hostels.

  • Q: What is the cancellation policy for hostel admissions?
    • Booking amount is nonrefundable.
    • Once a student is onboard no refund will be applicable
    • Student once onboarded will liable to pay the second installment, even if they cancel the admission/left in between Academic year.
    • All cancellation or refund requests must be submitted only through the official email. ( hostel.admin@mitwpu.edu.in)
    • Provide proof of fee payment, relevant documents, and the College cancellation email from the University.
    • Refund timeline: 45 to 60 working days
  • Q: Is the mess fee refundable?

    A: Mess fee is non-refundable unless the cancellation is within 0-20 days of joining.

  • Q: Can I change my room after allotment?

    A: Room changes are not allowed except in cases with prior approval from the warden.

  • Q: What items are provided in the room?

    A: Rooms are equipped with beds, mattresses, wardrobes, study tables, chairs, and attached /common washroom bucket and mug.

  • Q: Can I bring my own furniture or appliance?

    A: No additional furniture or electrical appliances are allowed without prior permission.

  • Q: Is there a policy for room maintenance?

    A: Students are responsible for maintaining their rooms. Inspections will be conducted to ensure cleanliness and compliance

  • Q: What are the mess timings?
    • A: 1. Breakfast:6:00 AM to 8:30 AM
    • 2. Dinner: 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM
    • 3. Lunch is provided only on weekends and public holidays from 1:30 PM to 3:00 PM.
    • 4. Tiffin’s services are not allowed; you can enjoy your meal at the cafeteria.
  • Q: Can students take food to their rooms?

    A: No, Except in medical emergencies with prior approval.

  • Q: How can students apply for the leave?

    A: Students must submit leave requests three days in advance on the students’ app/ email with parental approval.

  • Q: What is the procedure for vacating the hostel?

    A: Students must complete the no dues process signed by the warden and must inform in advance.

  • Q: Are Parents /guardians allowed to stay overnight?

    A: No parents /guardians are not allowed to stay overnight at the hostel.

  • Q: Is there any timings for visits?

    A: Visitors can visit the hostel from 9 AM to 9 PM.

  • Q: How the electricity bill will be charged?

    A: INR 300 is included in the package, above 300 residents have to pay the bill.

  • Q: Whom to pay the electricity bill?

    A: Amount has to be paid on the resident App, No cash transition to be made.

  • Q: Are there any rules regarding the noise levels?

    A: Strict silence must be observed from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM to maintain hostel decorum.

  • Q: Is parking available for students?

    A: Two–wheeler parking is available on a first come, first serve basis, subject to hostel parking availability.

  • Q: What should do in case of emergencies?

    A: Students should immediately inform the warden or student support staff.

  • Q: Are there any penalties for damaging the hostel property?

    A: Yes, students must bear the cost of repairs or replacement, and repeated violations any result in disciplinary action.

  • Contact Information

    Q: Whom should I contact for further inquiries?

    A: Email: hostel.admin@mitwpu.edu.in
