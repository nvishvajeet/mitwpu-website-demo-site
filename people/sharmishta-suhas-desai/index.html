Dr. Sharmishta Suhas Desai

Professor

Education

  1. Ph.D: Ph.D. In Computer Engineering- Savitribai Phule Pune University
  2. M.Tech: M.Tech Information Technology-MIT COE
  3. B.Tech: B.Tech Information Technology-Shivaji University

About

My teaching philosophy emphasizes experiential learning, research-driven education, and strong industry collaboration to prepare students for real-world challenges. I am motivated to cultivate innovation, critical thinking, and entrepreneurship among students through project-based learning and mentorship.

My contributions include securing funded research grants from ICMR,AICTE etc, leading innovative projects such as drone-based plant identification and portable agribots, and mentoring a student-led startup operating nationally and internationally.

Through research, industry partnerships, and innovation initiatives, I strive to support MIT World Peace University’s vision of value-based education, technological advancement, and creating socially responsible innovators.

Academic work

  • 22 years
  • 10 Years
  • Received ₹1.50 Crore research grant for the project titled “Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)” funded by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR).
  • Received approval for a ₹24 Lakhs research grant from the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) for the project titled “Intelligent Drones Assistance for Herbal Plant Labeling from Dense Hilly Forests for Environmentalists & Botanists and Crop Health Monitoring for Farmers.” The project focuses on drone-based imaging and AI techniques for plant identification and crop health monitoring.
  • Received a project grant under the IEEE Affordable Agri Lab initiative and led the development of a portable Agribot. The autonomous robot was designed to reduce farmers’ exposure to fertilizers and pesticides by enabling controlled and precise dispersion, thereby promoting safer and more efficient agricultural practices.
  • Executed a consultancy project worth ₹8.5 Lakhs with Bharat Forge Ltd., Pune, on “Inspection of Aircraft Engine Blades Using Computer Vision.”
  • Completed industry-oriented projects in collaboration with reputed organizations such as Škoda Auto Volkswagen India and Tata Technologies, strengthening industry–academia collaboration and providing practical exposure to students.
