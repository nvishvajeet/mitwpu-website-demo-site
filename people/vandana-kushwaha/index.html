Dr. Vandana Kushwaha

Assistant Professor

About

Currently Assistant Professor at the School of Economics and Commerce, Department of Commerce and Accounting, MIT WPU, with the experience of 5 years in Applied Economics domain. Doctorate in Applied Economics, Faculty of commerce, University of Lucknow, 2023. Thesis title “CONSUMPTION PATTERN OF ELECTRICITY IN RURAL AND URBAN AREAS: A CASE STUDY OF SONBHADRA AND LUCKNOW DISTRICT OF UTTAR PRADESH”. Also qualified for Lectureship, UGC NET - Commerce, 2019.

Additional information

  • Kushwaha V (2024): Profiling of OTT Viewers and their Perception Mapping using Advanced Technological Models, Volume: 5 No. 1, 791-809.
  • Kushwaha V (2020): Electricity Sector in India: Status, Problems and Future Challenges Kaav International Journal of Economics, Commerce & Business Management (KIJECBM), V7 (2020) 6-14.
  • Kushwaha V (2020): The Status of Rural Electrification Access and Progress of Uttar Pradesh, Solid State Technology (SST), Volume: 63 Issue: 1s Publication Year: 2020.
