Dr. Ganesh Bhaurao Choudhari

Assistant Professor

Education

  1. M. Pharm, PhD

About

Dr. Ganesh Bhaurao Choudhari is an Assistant Professor with a M. Pharm. in Pharmaceutical Chemistry and a submitted Ph. D. With over 12.6 years of experience in undergraduate academics, 7 years in postgraduate academics, and 3 years in industry, he is a seasoned professional in his field. He has 10 research publications, 4 review/special articles, and 3 patents to his credit. Dr. Choudhari has also presented 8 papers nationally, 9 papers internationally, and 4 papers regionally. He has attended 17 STTPs/Workshops/FDPs and has received research grants of 1.60 lakhs from BCUD, Savitribai Phule Pune University and research seed fund of 3 lakhs from Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune for his research projects. He has successfully completed over 15 consultancy projects and training sessions on flash chromatography.

Research interests

Formulation, standardisation and biological evaluation of herbal preparation(s) or allopathic preparation (s) Formulation optimisation and development by QbD and AQbD Approach Analytical method Development and validation for API and /or formulation Impurity profiling: degradation study of active pharmaceutical ingredients (API) or pharmaceutical dosage form Stability study of API and /or formulation Isolation of Phytoconstituents and degradant by flash chromatography In- Silico study and network pharmacology for herbal drug development
