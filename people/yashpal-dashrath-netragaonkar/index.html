Dr. Yashpal Dashrath Netragaonkar

Associate Professor

About

Dr. Yashpal Dashrath Netragaonkar is an Associate Professor in Education, who has served the field for over 21 years. His experience as a teacher spans across the levels of Secondary School and Higher Education.

He had conducted National Level conference in the year of 2014 on Innovative Practices in Teacher Education and International Conference in the year of 2016 on Empowering E-Generation: Vision 2020 He had participated and presented his research papers in 15 National seminars & 46 International Conference and 20 National Level workshops.

He is a Member of Professional Organization 1. Life Member of “The Learning Community” Association for Learners. Lucknow. U. P. India. 2. Life Member of Indian Association of Teacher Educators (IATE) (Y17) 3. Life Member of All India Association for Educational Research (AIAER) 4. Life Member of Council of Teacher Education (CTE)

Research interests

Teacher Education, Research in Education & Statistics
