Dr. Balaso Nivruti Jagdale

Associate Professor

Education

  1. Ph.D: Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology
  2. M.Tech: M.E. Computer Engineering at VJTI Mumbai
  3. B.Tech: B.E. In Computer Engineering - BVCOE, Pune

About

Balaso Jagdale received a Diploma in Industrial Electronics from Govt. Polytechnic Latur and BE Computer Engineering degree from Pune University in 1992. He received ME in Computer Engineering from VJTI, under Mumbai University in 1999. He has been awarded Ph.D. in the field of Computer Science and Technology from G H Raisoni College of Engineering affiliated to RTM Nagpur University, India.

He is presently working as Associate Professor, School of Computer Engineering and Technology at Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune. He has more than 33+ years of academics’ experience including head of the computer department at Sardar Patel College of Engineering, Mumbai.

He has 30 publications in journals, 24 in conference proceedings, and 05 book- Chapters in his credits. He has research interests in Computer Networks, Security, Internet of Things and blockchain Technology and Artificial Intelligence. He also has a Certified Ethical Hacker certification from EC Council. He is also associated with Govt. and University committees as subject expert.

He is a Member of IET Pune LN and life member of CSI, IETE, ISTE INDIA. Professional Affiliations: Member of IET Pune LN, Life member of CSI, IETE, ISTE INDIA.
