Dr. Mukesh Kumar Pal

Assistant Professor

About

Dr. Mukesh Kumar Pal is an Assistant Professor in the department Mathematics and Statistics, MIT-World Peace University, Kothrud, Pune, Maharashtra-411038, India. Dr. Pal obtained his Ph.D. & M.Phil. degree from Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines) Dhanbad, and master’s from Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi. Now, Dr. Pal working in the field of Electrodynamics; Mathematical modelling of Smart Materials; and Wave Propagation. He has published several research articles in reputed peer-reviewed SCI Journals and currently serving as a reviewer in peer-reviewed Journals, National & International conferences.

Research interests

Mathematical modelling of smart materials, elastodynamics, and wave propagation.
