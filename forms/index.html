Forms Central

Every form, and where to get it

One index of every form and letter MIT-WPU issues, for students and staff alike: what it is, who may ask, where the form actually lives, where it goes, what it costs and how long it takes.

Before you look for a form

This site hosts no forms, and never will

Nothing here collects a name, a registration number, a document or a payment. It is an index: it says what each form is and links out to wherever the university actually keeps it, the same split the fee tables and the admissions pages use.

That is a constraint on this demonstration rather than a comment on the university's arrangements. A service request carries a student's name, their registration number and often a scanned certificate, and a demonstration site has nowhere lawful to put any of it.

How to use this page

You arrive with an errand rather than a department. Where the forms actually are

Most of MIT-WPU's forms are not where anybody would look. Twenty-seven are printed as annexures inside six documents named for something else — a research manual, a hostel manual, two examination ordinances, an admission policy, a club handbook — and not one of the six filenames contains the word “form”.

The invention disclosure form is page 68 of a research manual. The hostel admission form is page 29 of a hostel manual. The complaint format for an examination is page 61 of the 2020 examination ordinances. Each is real, complete and printable, and each is reachable only by downloading a long PDF and scrolling to the back of it.

So every form here says which of four states it is in: an annexure inside a longer document, a request to an online system, something you do at a counter, or not published at all.

  • Examination Ordinances 2020 — 63 pages, published at /academics/ordinances as “Examination Ordinances 2020”. Its table of contents lists three annexures, two of which are forms.
    • Annexure I - Form of Unfair Means by student — page 57, what it is for
    • Annexure II - Format of Complaint — page 61, what it is for
  • Examination Ordinances 2017 — 43 pages, published at /academics/ordinances as “Examination Ordinances 2017”. Still the governing ordinance for backlog examinations of students admitted before 2022-23, so its form is still live.
    • Form of Undertaking — page 37, what it is for
  • Research Manual 2023-24 — 81 pages, published at /research/ipr as “Research Policy”. The IPR Cell page calls this the research policy and describes it as setting out the ownership, rights, responsibilities and procedures for intellectual property. Eleven complete forms are printed at the back of it and in its incentives chapter.
    • Application Form to claim incentive for Publications (Non-Open Access)/Book Chapters in Q1-Q4/ABDC Journals — page 18, what it is for
    • Application Form to claim Open Access Fee for Publication in Q1/Q2/A*/A Journals — page 19, what it is for
    • Application Form to claim expenses for participating in a Conference/Seminar/Workshop — page 20, what it is for
    • Note Sheet for Approval of Consultancy Projects — page 33, what it is for
    • Invention Disclosure Form — MIT-WPU/IPR-02 — page 68, what it is for
    • Application for Copyright Registration — MIT-WPU/IPR-03 — page 71, what it is for
    • IP Facilitation Request — MIT-WPU/IPR-04 — page 74, what it is for
  • Hostel Manual 2026-27 — 34 pages, published at /life-wpu/hostel-facilities as “Hostel Manual 2026-27”. Carries the hostel admission form and both UGC anti-ragging undertakings in its last six pages. The hostel page's own “Apply Now for Hostel” control points back at the hostel page; the actual form is in here.
    • Hostel Admission form — page 29, what it is for
  • Admission Policy 2026-27 — 101 pages, published at /student-information as “Admission Policy 2026-27”. Nine numbered proformas fill its last nine pages — the certificate formats a defence, disability, migrant or sponsored candidate has to produce. They are the most buried forms on the whole site.
    • Proforma–I to Proforma–IX — page 89, what it is for
  • Students' Club Handbook 2024-25 — 34 pages, published at /life-wpu/clubs as “Students' Club Handbook”. Names the club activity approval form as step three of running any club event.
    • Student Club Activity Approval Form — page 16, what it is for

Forms the university publishes nothing about

  • Rank certificate — Your position in the cohort, stated officially. No captured page mentions a rank certificate. The consolidated grade card carries a revised grade-point average but states no rank.
