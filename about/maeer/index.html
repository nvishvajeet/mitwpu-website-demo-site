Institutional foundation

MAEER

MIT began its academic journey under the Maharashtra Academy of Engineering and Educational Research (MAEER) in 1983. MIT World Peace University carries that educational legacy.

Founder President

Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad

Founder President, MIT-WPU; UNESCO Chairholder

Founder and Chief Patron of MAEER’s MIT Group of Institutions, Founder-President of the World Peace Centre at Alandi, and creator of the World Peace Dome.

MAEER mission

Education in service of society and peace

MAEER brings education, innovation, and culture together with a public commitment to science, spirituality, and the pursuit of world peace.

World Peace Centre · Alandi

Since 1998

UNESCO Chair for Human Rights, Democracy and Peace

The World Peace Centre at Alandi was established in 1996. The UNESCO Chair for Human Rights, Democracy and Peace was established at MAEER in 1998, with Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad as chairholder. In 2019, UNESCO New Delhi and MIT-WPU jointly organised the Second Annual Meeting of UNESCO Chairs in India in Pune.

Legacy

Four decades of education

A concise record drawn from the university’s published history.

  1. 1981–1983

    An institution conceived

    MIT was conceived as an institution dedicated to innovation in education.

  2. 1983

    The inaugural year

    MIT began its academic journey and welcomed its first Electronics and Telecommunication students.

  3. 1996–2003

    World Peace Centre and UNESCO Chair

    The World Peace Centre at Alandi was established in 1996. The UNESCO Chair for Human Rights, Democracy and Peace followed at MAEER in 1998, and the institution continued its public work in education and peace.

  4. 2004–2014

    New national initiatives

    The Bharat Asmita National Awards, Vishwashanti Gurukul, and Bharatiya Chhatra Sansad expanded the institution’s public work.

  5. 2015–2019

    Expanding horizons

    The institution expanded its educational presence, and the World Peace Dome at Loni, Pune, became a symbol of its commitment to peace.

  6. Since 2021

    A university in transformation

    MIT-WPU has continued its digital transformation while carrying forward its commitment to education, peace, and innovation.
