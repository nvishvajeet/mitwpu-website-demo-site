MIT-WPU Shines on the International Stage!

We are happy to share that MIT-WPU’s Chem E Car team represented India at the ASC 2024 in San Diego, competing in the renowned Chem E Car competition. This unique event challenges teams to design cars powered solely by chemical reactions for both movement and stopping—pushing innovation and scientific knowledge to new heights.

We are happy to share that MIT-WPU’s Chem E Car team represented India at the ASC 2024 in San Diego, competing in the renowned Chem E Car competition.

This unique event challenges teams to design cars powered solely by chemical reactions for both movement and stopping—pushing innovation and scientific knowledge to new heights.

Adding to the pride, MIT-WPU was honored with the Outstanding Student Chapter Award for the 6th consecutive year by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers. This international recognition reflects our students’ commitment to excellence, creativity, and leadership in the field.

Congratulations to the entire team for showcasing India’s talent and dedication on a global platform!
