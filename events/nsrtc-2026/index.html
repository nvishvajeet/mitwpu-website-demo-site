NSRTC 2026

A three-day conclave on science, technology, and innovation

Date
17–19 July 2026
Location
MIT World Peace University, Pune
Format
Conference
Status
Past event
Official MIT-WPU event report ↗

Overview

Scientists, researchers, industry leaders, policymakers, innovators, and students met for three days of interdisciplinary discussion on emerging research and technology.

The opening day examined the work recognised by the Nobel Prize and the Queen Elizabeth Prize for Engineering. The inaugural programme included keynote addresses, awards for contributions to research and technological innovation, and a special lecture on generating national wealth through science and technology.

The following two days combined parallel technical sessions with round-table discussions, poster presentations, project exhibitions, and a Scholars-to-Scientists Connect initiative. Contributors came from universities, national laboratories, research institutes, industry, and public organisations.

Themes

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Sustainable energy and advanced materials
  • Semiconductors and quantum technologies
  • Healthcare technologies and biotechnology
  • Indigenous innovation

Programme

Day 1

Prizes, inauguration, and awards

Sessions considered the premise of the Nobel Prize and the Queen Elizabeth Prize for Engineering. The inaugural programme included keynote addresses, the Vigyan Maharshi Award, the DeepTech Young Entrepreneur Awards, and a special lecture on generation of national wealth through science and technology.

Days 2–3

Parallel scientific programme

Four research tracks ran alongside round-table discussions, poster presentations, project exhibitions, and Scholars-to-Scientists Connect.

Closing session

Valedictory

The conference closed by recognising the contributions of speakers, participants, and organisers.

  • CSIR laboratories

  • Other research and industry organisations

Awards

Vigyan Maharshi Award

Conferred during the inaugural programme for contribution to science and research.

DeepTech Young Entrepreneur Awards

Conferred for achievement in technological innovation and entrepreneurship.

Outcomes

  • Round-table discussions connected academic, industry, and young-researcher perspectives.
  • Poster presentations and project exhibitions gave researchers and students a forum to present work.
  • Scholars-to-Scientists Connect created direct interaction between early-career researchers and established scientists.
