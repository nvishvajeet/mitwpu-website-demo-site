NSRTC 2025

Science, innovation, and national research priorities

Date
Three-day conference · 2025
Location
MIT World Peace University, Pune
Format
Conference
Status
Past event
Official MIT-WPU event report ↗

Overview

A three-day national meeting of scientists, technologists, policy leaders, and students organised around scientific achievement, cross-disciplinary research, and national research priorities.

The opening programme considered Nobel Prize-recognised work in physiology or medicine, chemistry, physics, and engineering. Plenary speakers included Shekhar Mande, Vijayamohanan Pillai, Lakshmanan Muthusamy, and Deepak Ranade.

A formal inaugural ceremony brought together Vijay Bhatkar, N. Kalaiselvi, Ashutosh Sharma, Ashok Joshi, Prasad Khandekar, Milind Pande, and Vishwanath D. Karad. Kalaiselvi, Director General of CSIR, addressed students on pursuing excellence in professional work and in life.

Themes

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Advanced materials and processing
  • Digital transformation, energy, and sustainability
  • Healthcare, pharmaceutical science, and biotechnology

Research tracks

Artificial Intelligence

AI with cryptology, materials design, multilingual applications, responsible AI systems, digital twins, and AI in scientific discovery.

Contributors: Bimal Kumar Roy, Sourav Pal, Radhakrishna Ganti, Yogesh Wadadekar, Amol Dhondse

Advanced Materials and Processing

Two-dimensional materials, CMOS and MEMS systems, clay-based electrolytes, carbon-dioxide conversion, and two-dimensional superconductors.

Contributors: V. Ramgopal Rao, Satishchandra Ogale, U. V. Waghmare, Vijayamohan Pillai, Sharad Lande, Bappi Paul

Digital Transformations in Energy and Sustainability

Photonic circuits, quantum imaging, scalable energy solutions, fusion energy, and hydrogen syngas.

Contributors: Shailendra Varshney, Rajnish Kumar, G. D. Yadav, Dinesh K. Aswal

Healthcare, Pharmaceutical Science, and Biotechnology

Infection control, nucleic-acid therapies, One Health, metabolic health, tuberculosis drug discovery, and plant-based bioactives.

Contributors: Ashok C. Khandkar, Pragya D. Yadav, Sanjay B. Maggirwar, Umesh Varshney, Ashok Giri, Pramod Tripathi

Programme

Day 1

Plenaries and inauguration

Plenary sessions examined Nobel Prize-recognised work across medicine, chemistry, physics, and engineering. The day continued with three special talks and the inaugural ceremony.

Day 2

Technical sessions

Four parallel tracks covered artificial intelligence, advanced materials, energy and sustainability, and health and biotechnology. Round-table discussions and poster presentations connected young researchers with invited scientists.

Day 3

Advanced sessions and valedictory

The four tracks continued with research on responsible AI, new materials, clean energy, metabolic health, and drug discovery. A valedictory session at Swami Vivekananda Auditorium included awards for outstanding poster presentations.

Awards

Vigyan Maharshi Award

Jayant Vishnu Narlikar

Conferred posthumously for contributions to science and cosmology.

Outstanding poster presentation awards

Presented during the valedictory ceremony; the retained report does not name the recipients.

Outcomes

  • Young researchers presented posters and discussed their work directly with invited scientists.
  • Round-table sessions connected fundamental research, applied science, technology development, and national capacity.
  • The closing discussion reflected on interdisciplinary research, scientific development, and the next generation of researchers.
