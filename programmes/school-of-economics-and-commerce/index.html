Programmes

School Of Economics And Commerce

What sets us apart is our comprehensive curriculum, designed to encompass the diverse facets of economics, finance, accounting, marketing, and business management. Our approach goes beyond textbooks and lectures – you'll dive into real-world case studies, simulations, and hands-on projects to hone your analytical skills and problem-solving abilities. We've created an environment that goes beyond the classroom, empowering you with knowledge, practical skills, and experiences that are vital for success in the ever-evolving fields of economics and commerce. Your journey with us is not just about learning; it's about growing, thriving, and becoming the future leaders in these dynamic disciplines.

School of Economics and Commerce brings together the Department of Commerce & Accounting and the Department of Economics. Their programmes are listed below, with links to detailed eligibility, curriculum and fee information for each.

Department of Commerce & Accounting

The Department of Commerce and Accounting at MIT-WPU is committed to cultivating skilled professionals who possess a comprehensive understanding of financial analysis, tax management, statutory accounting, statistical analysis, and other critical competencies essential in the finance sector.

  • B.Com (Hons.)
  • B.Com Advanced Accounting and Auditing (Hons.)
  • B.Com Financial Analysis (Hons.)
  • B.Com Fintech (Hons.)
  • B.Com International Accounting and Finance (Hons.)
  • M.Com.
  • Ph.D. in Commerce

Department of Economics

With a strong focus on developing true leaders and collaborative team players, the department equips students with essential skills required for a seamless transition into the corporate world and public service. Offering a comprehensive range of academic programs—including undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. degrees in Economics —the department caters to diverse interests and career aspirations.

  • B.Sc. Economics (Hons.)
  • M.Sc. Economics
  • Ph.D. in Economics
