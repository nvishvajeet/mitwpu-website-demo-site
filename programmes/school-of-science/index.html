Programmes

School Of Science

The School of Science and Environmental Studies at MIT-WPU offers a diverse range of programmes that combine the study of scientific principles with a focus on environmental sustainability. The School offers numerous specialisations in wildlife, sustainability and regulations. The School collaborates with industry and corporate organisations, research centres and NGOs to provide students with exposure to CSR and Environmental Civic Liability to understand the market trends and demands.

MIT-WPU School of Science & Environmental Studies brings together the Department of Biosciences and Technology, the Department of Chemistry, the Department of Environmental Studies, the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, and the Department of Physics. Their programmes are listed below, with links to detailed eligibility, curriculum and fee information for each.

Department of Biosciences and Technology

The Department of Biosciences & Technology offers industry-oriented undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes that focus on contemporary and futuristic technical and scientific knowledge in the fields of biological sciences, and biotechnology.

  • Integrated M.Sc. Biotechnology
  • M.Sc. Biotechnology
  • M.Sc. Microbiology
  • Ph.D. in Biochemistry
  • Ph.D. in Biotechnology
  • Ph.D. in Microbiology

Department of Chemistry

The Department of Chemistry at MIT-WPU upholds a steadfast commitment to excellence in the realm of chemistry education and research. With the objective of reinforcing the fundamental principles of chemistry, the department blends theoretical and practical facets, facilitating comprehensive learning.

  • B.Sc. Chemistry (Hons)
  • M.Sc. Chemistry (Industrial Polymer Chemistry)
  • M.Sc. Chemistry (Organic Chemistry)

Department of Environmental Studies

The Department of Environmental Science at MIT-WPU has a resolute mission to cultivate the professionals of tomorrow, individuals who are dedicated to contributing towards the betterment of our planet through informed decision-making and active engagement in environmental preservation.

  • M.Sc. Environment Science
  • Ph.D. in Environmental Science

Department of Mathematics and Statistics

The Department of Mathematics and Statistics is a dynamic academic hub that embraces a holistic approach to education, integrating traditional quantitative disciplines with courses in peace studies and yoga. Our commitment extends beyond technical expertise, fostering the comprehensive development of students.

  • B.Sc. Applied Statistics and Data Analytics (Hons)
  • M.Sc. Mathematics and Data Science
  • M.Sc. Applied Statistics
  • Ph.D. in Mathematics
  • Ph.D. in Statistics

Department of Physics

  • B.Sc. Physics (Hons)
  • M.Sc. Physics
  • Ph.D. in Physics
