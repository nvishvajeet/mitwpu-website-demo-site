University services

Report a problem, request a service

What to do when something is broken, something is missing, or something has gone wrong — and who owns the answer.

Before you start

Look for the answer here first

Most of what people ring somebody about is written down. Read the answer below before you raise anything with anyone: the best request is the one nobody needed to make, and the teams here are small.

There is no single service desk. The university publishes no one number for everything, so each service is reached separately, and where it has published nothing this page says so rather than guessing.

How to use this page

Look for the thing that has gone wrong, not for the department that owns it. The owner is named inside the answer, because you should not have to know the organisation chart to report a broken air conditioner.

Computing is not here. The network, your accounts, email, software, research computing and the security of all of it have their own pages, because those questions are jobs somebody sits down to do rather than faults somebody reports, and they want the commands on the page.

Something is broken

A fault in a room, a building or a piece of equipment.

  • Something is broken in a teaching room, a laboratory or an office

    Email the Office of Infra & Facilities. Say which building, which floor and which room, and what is wrong with it.

    This is the only route the university publishes for a fault anywhere on campus. There is no fault-reporting system, no maintenance number and no counter to walk up to.

    Who owns this: Office of Infra & Facilities. The office named for campus infrastructure and facilities in the university's own directory of administrative offices.

  • Something is broken in my hostel room

    Tell the warden or the student support staff at your hostel straight away, and email the hostel administration if it is not fixed.

    Report it early. The hostel rules say a resident pays for repairs or replacement of what they damage, and a fault left unreported can become one of those.

    Who owns this: Hostel administration. The team that runs the residences at Ambegaon, Bavdhan, Karvenagar and Kothrud, their mess, their laundry and the shuttle between them and the campus.

  • A room, a washroom or a corridor has not been cleaned

    The university has not published who to tell. Housekeeping is described as a service the residences provide, but no team, address or number is given for it anywhere.

    Until an owner is published, the nearest route is the Office of Infra & Facilities for the campus and the hostel administration for a residence.

Your record, your results and your certificates

Transcripts, grade cards, degree certificates and verification.

  • I need a transcript, a duplicate grade card or a certificate

    This is a service request to the Department of Examinations, and it is made on the university's request form rather than by email.

    Allow time and expect a charge. Processing a transcript takes at least five working days, and separate fees are published for the document and for posting it.

    Who owns this: Examination Helpdesk. The Department of Examinations' public helpdesk. It is the only support service on the university's website that publishes a telephone number, an email address, a room and its opening hours together.

  • I need a university identity card, or mine is lost

    Nothing is published. The identity card is required — the library will not admit you without it and treats it as your membership card — but no page says who issues one, where, or what to do when it is lost.

    This is one of the more consequential gaps on this page, because the card is a precondition for a service that is otherwise well documented.

    The library states that the university identity card is mandatory to visit it and is treated as the membership card. Published at /life-wpu/library

Money

Fees, refunds and loans.

  • I have a question about my fees

    There is an Accounts Helpdesk, but you cannot reach it from a computer: on the university's student assistance page it is published as a QR code image, with no telephone number or email address in the text beside it.

    The published route that can actually be read is the Office of Finance & Accounts. A screen reader gets nothing at all from those QR codes, so this is an accessibility failure as much as a routing one.

    Who owns this: Office of Finance & Accounts. Budgeting, fee accounting and payments. A separate Accounts Helpdesk exists for students, but its contact details are published only inside a QR code image.

Living on campus

Rooms, meals, laundry, the shuttle and parking.

  • I have a question about my room, the mess, laundry or the shuttle

    The hostel administration answers all of these on one address and one number, and cancellations and refunds must go through that email address rather than any other route.

    The residences are off the main campus and a shuttle runs between them, so a hostel question and a transport question are usually the same question.

    Who owns this: Hostel administration. The team that runs the residences at Ambegaon, Bavdhan, Karvenagar and Kothrud, their mess, their laundry and the shuttle between them and the campus.

  • How do I get to campus, and where do I park?

    Beyond the hostel shuttle, nothing is published. There is no transport office, no route map, no timetable and no campus parking authority. The only parking the university describes is two-wheeler parking at the hostels, on a first-come basis.

    Two-wheeler parking at the hostels is available on a first come, first served basis, subject to availability. Published at /life-wpu/hostel-facilities

Health, safety and wellbeing

A doctor, a counsellor, and an emergency.

  • I need to see a doctor, or there is a medical emergency

    The university describes a medical service on campus but publishes no way to reach it: no number, no room, no hours.

    In an emergency, use the public emergency services. This page will not pretend to a number it does not have — and of every gap listed here, this is the one that most needs closing.

    Who owns this: Campus medical service. The university describes a full-time doctor, a 24-hour pharmacy, a three-bed facility, ambulances, ECG and day care. It publishes no way to reach any of them.

Books, rooms and equipment

Borrowing, booking and the things you have to reserve.

  • I want to borrow a book, or I have lost one

    Go in with your identity card, which is also your membership card. Five books for fifteen days is the standing entitlement, and a renewal means bringing the book back in person.

    A lost book is to be reported to the Librarian immediately, and either replaced or paid for at the current price. The library publishes no email address and no telephone number, so reporting it means going there.

    Who owns this: Knowledge Resource Centre. The university library, in its own building. It publishes its hours, its borrowing rules and what happens if you lose a book, but no way to ask it anything.

If something has gone seriously wrong

Complaints, harassment, ragging and discrimination are not service requests, and they have their own statutory routes.

  • I need to make a formal complaint

    Do not use anything on this page. The university has statutory committees for grievances, ragging, harassment and discrimination, each with its own address and its own procedure, and each is the correct and protected route for what it covers.

    They are listed in the footer of every page on this site and are not restated here: a second, differently worded route to a statutory committee is worse than one.
