Step by step How to apply Six steps from registration and entrance testing to the final offer.

The application for Indian students runs on the MIT-WPU admissions portal, from registration to joining. The full step-by-step guide, including score routes for engineering, management, and law, is on the application-process page.

  1. Register online

    Create an account at admissions.mitwpu.edu.in and submit the registration form. You receive a login ID and password and land on your student dashboard.

  2. Fill the application form

    Complete your personal and academic profile and pay the application fee of Rs. 2,000.

  3. Take the MIT-WPU CET

    Your entrance-test link and date appear on the dashboard. Appear for the CET, or submit a valid national or state entrance score where that route is accepted.

  4. Merit list & provisional offer

    Check the merit list on your dashboard. On selection, you receive a provisional offer letter.

  5. Pay & verify

    Complete the first fee instalment, then payment, personal, education, and document verification.

  6. Final offer & join

    Receive your final offer letter and PRN, submit original documents, and join. Welcome to MIT-WPU.

Before you apply. The names of the candidate and parents must match the class-10 certificate exactly, with no prefixes. Marks from the qualifying examination and any national or state entrance test (MHT-CET / MIT-WPU CET) must be uploaded within 15 days of the results being announced, or the application will not be considered. Engineering admissions also accept MHT-CET and JEE (Main) scores; MBA accepts CAT, MAT, and XAT; Law accepts CLAT and MH-CET Law with the MIT-WPU personal interaction.

Entrance test & key dates MIT-WPU CET 2026 A 150-mark online entrance route, with programme dates and sample papers.

MIT-WPU CET 2026 is the university’s entrance exam for UG and PG admissions. It is conducted fully online and can be attempted from home. The Computer-Based Test covers general knowledge, logical reasoning, basic mathematics, English, and computer fundamentals; some streams add subject sections, a drawing test, or a portfolio.

  • 150total marks
  • 100Computer-Based Test (CBT)
  • 50Personal Interaction (PI)

Questions Admissions FAQs Six quick answers covering CET, fees, scores, scholarships, and applicant routes.

Quick answers to common admissions questions. For examination, grade-card, and academic queries, see the academics FAQ.

What is MIT-WPU CET 2026?

It is the university’s entrance exam for UG and PG admissions, conducted online. It carries 150 marks — a 100-mark Computer-Based Test and a 50-mark Personal Interaction — and can be attempted from home. See the CET page for the syllabus and sample papers.

How much is the application fee?

The application form fee is Rs. 2,000, paid on the admissions portal when you complete the form. See the application process for the full sequence.

Which other entrance scores does MIT-WPU accept?

Engineering admissions also accept MHT-CET and JEE (Main) scores; MBA accepts CAT, MAT, and XAT; Law accepts CLAT and MH-CET Law, with the MIT-WPU Personal Interaction. Qualifying scores must be uploaded within 15 days of results.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. MIT-WPU offers merit scholarships worth INR 50 crore for AY 2026–27 — 100% (5% of intake), 50% (up to 10%) and 25% (up to 12%) fee waivers, plus sports scholarships. See scholarships and scholarship details.

Can diploma holders get direct second-year entry?

Yes. Candidates with a three-year engineering diploma can apply for B.Tech direct second-year (lateral) entry, subject to eligibility. See the lateral-entry brochure.

How do international students apply?

International applicants apply through the Study-in-India route, which covers how to apply, visa guidance, and international scholarships. Start at International at MIT-WPU.
