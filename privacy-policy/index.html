Data protection

Privacy and compliance

The public website refuses non-essential tracking by default.

Last updated: 25 July 2026.

Who is responsible

MIT World Peace University is responsible for personal data processed through this public website. The university is located at Survey No. 124, Paud Road, Kothrud, Pune, Maharashtra 411038, India.

Interim privacy contact: Mr. Pravin Raut, Deputy Director of IT. Privacy enquiries and rights requests may be addressed to Mr. Raut through the university contact directory or by calling +91 20 7117 7104.

Compliance position

This public website is designed and operated to comply with applicable privacy and electronic-communications requirements, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection framework and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and ePrivacy rules.

The current release applies data minimisation, purpose-specific collection notices, no non-essential tracking by default, positive choice before optional third-party content loads, limited access-log retention, secure authenticated sessions, and a process for rights and grievance requests. These controls are checked before deployment and reviewed when the site, its services, or applicable requirements change.

Public browsing

The public website contains no advertising pixels, behavioural profiling, session replay, social-media trackers, or analytics tags. Its hosting service may process an IP address, requested URL, time, response status, browser identifier, and security signals to deliver the page, prevent abuse, and investigate faults. Routine access logs are scheduled for deletion within 30 days. Records needed for a documented security incident or legal obligation are kept only for that purpose and then deleted.

Cookies and device storage

The public website sets no non-essential cookies. It stores a colour-theme preference in the browser only after a visitor changes the theme. That preference stays on the device until the visitor clears it and is not sent to MIT-WPU. Signed-in university services may use essential session and security cookies to authenticate a user, prevent request forgery, and maintain the requested service. Those cookies are not used for advertising.

Because non-essential storage is disabled, the public website does not show a consent banner. If analytics or another non-essential service is proposed later, it must remain off until the visitor makes a specific choice. Refusal must be as easy as acceptance, and withdrawal must be available from every page.

External video

YouTube videos do not load automatically. A video connects to YouTube only after the visitor chooses Play video. At that point Google may receive connection data, including the visitor’s IP address, under Google’s own privacy terms. Visitors can refuse by leaving the video unloaded and can use the accompanying link in a separate browser context if preferred.

Forms and applications

Admissions, employment, research, event, and other forms must provide a notice at the point of collection. That notice must identify the information requested, why it is needed, the lawful basis, recipients, retention period, whether it is required, and how to exercise rights. This public-site notice does not authorize a form to collect additional information.

Legal bases and sharing

Public pages and security logs are operated to provide the requested service and for the university’s legitimate interests in reliable and secure publishing. Consent is used where a visitor chooses optional third-party content. Personal data is shared only with processors or authorities needed for the stated purpose and subject to applicable safeguards. MIT-WPU does not sell public-site visitor data.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, a person may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or an explanation of processing; object to processing; withdraw consent; nominate another person; or raise a grievance. A person in the European Economic Area may also complain to the data-protection authority where they live, work, or believe an infringement occurred. A request will not be treated less favourably because optional tracking was refused.

International access

The university is established in India and the public site is available internationally. Where European data-protection law applies, transfers and service-provider access must use an applicable transfer mechanism and safeguards. Mr. Pravin Raut is the interim privacy contact. MIT-WPU will publish any additional data-protection-officer or European-representative details required for a particular processing activity.

Changes

Material changes will be dated on this page. A new optional purpose will not be enabled on the basis of an earlier choice.
