International

A front door for the world — international admissions and eligibility, fees and scholarships, global partnerships and MoUs, study abroad and immersion, and the visa, arrival, and support MIT-WPU’s international students need.

International directory

Admissions & eligibility

MIT-WPU admits Foreign National, OCI, NRI and CIWGC/CIWSEA applicants through its International Education Centre, under UGC norms and AIU equivalence, and is a partner of the Government of India’s Study in India programme.

Study in India→Why India→How to Apply→Student Testimonials→Admissions overview→Programmes→

Fees & scholarships

Merit scholarships of 50% (FN, OCI, CIWGC and CIWSEA) and up to 100% for Foreign Nationals on the programme fee; the application form fee is Rs. 1,800, with programme fees confirmed at offer stage.

International Scholarships→Scholarships (all)→Student loans→

Global partnerships & MoUs

MIT-WPU’s International Collaboration Department reports partnerships with more than 30 institutions worldwide; recent announcements are below.

Partner Universities→MoU with Michigan State University→Collaboration with USC & Sussex→Ties with Zahira College, Colombo→

Study abroad & exchange

Summer and winter schools and student exchange with partner universities — short, typically two-to-three-week engagements built around domain, cross-cultural, and industry exposure.

Study Abroad Overview→International Programmes→Director’s Message→

International immersion

Recent immersion programmes have taken MIT-WPU students to universities and innovation hubs across Asia, Europe, and beyond.

International Immersion→Engineering ecosystems in China→National University of Singapore→Immersion in Japan→GITEX GLOBAL 2025, Dubai→

Visa, arrival & support

Apply for a student visa endorsed to MIT-WPU, then register with the FRRO/FRO Pune within 14 days of arrival; the International Education Centre assists with reporting, airport pickup, and settling in.
