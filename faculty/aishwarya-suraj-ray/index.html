Dr. Aishwarya Suraj Ray

Ms. Aishwarya Ray is an accomplished academic and marketing professional with over 8 years of experience in teaching and entrepreneurship.

Assistant Professor

Bio

Ms. Aishwarya Ray is an accomplished academic and marketing professional with over 8 years of experience in teaching and entrepreneurship. She brings a unique blend of academic expertise and industry-oriented insights, enabling students to bridge the gap between marketing theory and real- world business practices.

She holds three master's degrees and a Ph.D. in Marketing, reflecting her strong academic foundation and commitment to lifelong learning. She is also a recipient of the prestigious Summer Research Faculty Fellowship from IIT Delhi. Ms. Ray has actively contributed to the academic community by serving as Convener and Co-convener for several national and international conferences, facilitating knowledge exchange among academicians, researchers, and industry professionals. She has also successfully organized multiple Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs) aimed at enhancing teaching effectiveness and research capabilities.

Her teaching and research interests include Marketing Management, Consumer Behaviour, Digital Marketing, Branding and Strategic Management. Passionate about experiential learning, she strives to create an engaging classroom environment that encourages critical thinking, innovation, and industry readiness among students.
