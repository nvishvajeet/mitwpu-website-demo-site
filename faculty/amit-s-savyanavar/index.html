Bio

Dr. Amit Savyanavar is an Associate Professor working in the Department of Computer Engineering and Technology at Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune. He is a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering and has a teaching experience of 25 years. He is a researcher specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning, with a strong focus on applied AI.

His research interests include AI and deep learning architectures, with contributions through publications, academic collaborations, and student-led research initiatives.

He actively engages in industry-academia interaction, promotes research-oriented learning, and mentors UG, PG & Ph.D. students in translating theoretical concepts into impactful real-world solutions. Professional Affiliations: CSE Life Member

Specialisation: Computer Science and Engineering

Areas of Expertise: Operating Systems, Computer Vision, Mobile Grid computing
