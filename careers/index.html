Work with us

Research faculty at the Central Research Facility

The Central Research Facility (CRF) brings materials characterization, thin-film fabrication, and analysis together in one place. We are recruiting one or two early-career faculty to make it their own — to run the facility, publish from it, mentor doctoral students, and build it into a regional centre.

The facility you would lead

A commissioned suite of high-end instruments spanning characterization, fabrication and analysis — the platform is ready; it needs a scientist to build a programme around it.

  • FE-SEM + EDS
  • X-ray diffractometer (XRD / GIXRD)
  • ICP-MS
  • Raman spectroscopy
  • RF–DC sputtering
  • Thermal & e-beam deposition
  • Battery / coin-cell fabrication
  • Nanoindenter
  • Tribometer
  • Surface profiler
  • Particle / zeta size analyser
  • UV-Vis / DRS / NIR spectroscopy

See the Central Research Facility for the full instrument list and capabilities.

Who we are looking for

Early-career experimentalists whose research depends on these instruments — who want to build a facility and a group, not just use one. Assistant Professor applicants are especially encouraged; strong mid-career candidates are welcome too.

Materials Science — thin films & nanomaterials

Assistant Professor · early-career

An experimentalist in thin-film and nanomaterials research — energy materials and batteries, functional coatings, or nanoscale devices — whose work centres on deposition and FE-SEM / XRD / Raman / nanoindentation.

Analytical / Physical Chemistry

Assistant Professor · early-career

A chemist whose research is instrument-intensive on ICP-MS and optical spectroscopy — trace and elemental analysis, electrochemistry, or materials chemistry.

  • A PhD with postdoctoral experience and a track record built on hands-on characterization.
  • A clear research vision that uses the CRF instruments as its core platform.
  • A commitment to building the facility, its user base, and a doctoral group.

What MIT-WPU offers

  • The CRF as your platform from day one — no waiting to build.
  • Start-up support, doctoral students, and industry and consultancy channels.
  • Freedom to set the facility's scientific direction and its external users.

About MIT-WPU

Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune, is a research-active private university with a four-decade legacy, schools across engineering, sciences, and humanities, and a large doctoral community. Learn more about the university and its research.

Submit a CV and a one-page research statement describing how you would use and grow the CRF, through the MIT-WPU careers portal.
