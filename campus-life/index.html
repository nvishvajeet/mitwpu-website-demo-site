Student voices

1995

Mr. K. V. Karthik Managing Director, Deccan Industries, Coimbatore

Graduate

My journey at the Maharashtra Institute of Technology (MIT) remains one of the most defining phases of my life. I joined MIT in 1995 to pursue Mechanical Engineering, stepping away from home for the first time. The initial days were challenging, but the support of my professors and peers helped me adapt and grow in confidence. MIT’s greatest strength lies in building a solid foundation in engineering fundamentals. It taught me that true engineering is about understanding concepts, not memorizing them. The emphasis on basics, analytical thinking, and problem-solving continues to guide me even today in my professional journey. Beyond academics, MIT instilled discipline, responsibility, and a spirit of healthy competition. It shaped me into a confident engineer, ready to take on real-world challenges. If I were to sum up MIT in one word, it would be “Confidence” — the confidence to learn, adapt, and succeed.

2004

Chaitanya Joshi Senior Director – Technical Sales & Strategy

Graduate

It gives me immense pride to reflect on my journey as an alumna of the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, School of Health Sciences and Technology, Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune. Graduating in 2004, I carry with me not only strong pharmaceutical knowledge but also the values, leadership qualities, and curiosity nurtured during my time here. The dedicated faculty and academic rigor helped build a solid foundation, inspiring us to think critically and strive for excellence beyond the classroom. The vibrant campus life, group projects, practical training, and lifelong friendships made those years truly unforgettable. Every lesson and challenge contributed significantly to my personal and professional growth. Today, as Senior Director – Technical Sales & Strategy at OmniActive Health Technologies Ltd., I truly recognize how this institution has played an instrumental role in shaping my confidence, global perspective, and commitment to continuous learning. I remain deeply grateful to the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, School of Health Sciences and Technology, Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune, for empowering me with the skills and vision to excel in the global business landscape. I extend my sincere appreciation and best wishes to the future torchbearers of this institution.

2017

Aniruddha Atigre

Graduate

“I have witnessed an enormous growth curve in myself, not just in academics, but in every aspect of my life while studying in a positive, lively, and peaceful environment at the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering, MIT-WPU. Engagement in innovative projects at such an excellent infrastructure having great facilities for design and testing has been indeed a really life-transforming experience for me. These projects made me understand the application of the knowledge I was acquiring. Moreover, I loved the Peace Studies module which has contributed greatly in the development of my personality. “
MIT World Peace University
Pune, India
