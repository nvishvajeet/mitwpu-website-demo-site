Published White Paper with Cybage Software Pvt. Ltd., Pune. Received IEEE Best paper award at International Conference on Data, Energy and Communication (IC-DECoN 2025) at OIST, Bhopal. Published a book chapter in the book Advancing Engineering and Technology through Interdisciplinary Approaches, published by Cambridge Scholars Publishing, UK. A catalogue record for the book is available from the British Library.

Prof. Dr. Shubhalaxmi Joshi and Ph.D. Scholar Chinmay Patwardhan

They have received approval from Fujitsu Japan for Fujitsu Quantum Simulator usage subscription worth $100,000/- for research work.

Conducted Visioning Conference at NIT, Raipur on Transdisciplinary Education. Invited internships through Industry Connects with more than 20 Industries viz. Calfus Technologies, Bentley Systems, Patseer, Veonit, Zensar, Infosys, Proxie Studio, Samprama Digitek, Partex etc. Initiated MoUs with companies like Bentley Systems, AI-Shala, Patseer etc.

Served as Session Chair in two international conferences. Successfully facilitated one MoU and one hackathon with 1,00,000 Rupees Sponsorship.

Won the Young Researcher Award in Hospitality and Tourism at the 4th Student Conclave in Bengaluru.

Dr. Anand Kulkarni, MIT-WPU, edited a Springer Nature handbook on AI and optimization methods in under 3 years a milestone of achievement. It's a 2-volume, 15-section resource with contributions from 125 experts.

Dr. Anand J. Kulkarni, has published a textbook titled Optimization Methods and Algorithms with Springer. This remarkable textbook is a truly comprehensive resource that covers all aspects of optimization concepts and terminology. It provides clear explanations, illustrative examples, and real-world problems with solutions, making it a valuable reference for students, researchers, and professionals alike.

Dr. Bharat Kale led an insightful International Conference on Bridging Ancient and Advanced Technology, exploring how ancient Vedic methods can be applied to create advanced materials like nanomaterials and oxides.
