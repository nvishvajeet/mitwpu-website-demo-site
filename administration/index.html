Administration

University offices and how to reach them

The offices of MIT-WPU, what each one is for, and the published route to it — with the offices the university has published no route to named rather than left out.

If it is urgent

The university's public website publishes no route to campus security and no route to the campus medical service. Both numbers below were found in the source of its contact page, where they had been commented out and were never shown to a reader.

They are the two most important rows on this page and they are the two least certain. Read the note beneath them before relying on either.

Campus security and the campus medical service. Neither has a published contact anywhere else on this site.

Campus Security

The university's security supervisor, for anything happening on campus now. MIT-WPU Security Supervisor: not yet published. The source names the post and not the person, which for this office is the right way round. No email address is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published. No security office appears in the university's own directory of administrative offices, and no security contact appears on any of the roughly 1,200 routes captured from the public site.

Telephone — MIT-WPU Security Supervisor — (in the captured source, never published) +91 9322615464

Campus Medical Service

Vishwaraj Clinic on the MIT-WPU campus: a full-time doctor, a three-bed facility, a 24-hour pharmacy and ambulances. No email address is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published. The published medical facilities page describes a 24-hour ambulance service and gives no number to call for it.

Telephone — Vishwaraj Clinic, MIT-WPU Campus — (in the captured source, never published) +91 7276056060

No office in this part of the university is recorded yet.

Read this before using the numbers

Some of these numbers were never published by the university

MIT-WPU's contact page carries a table of eleven departmental telephone numbers inside an HTML comment. The table does not render and no visitor to the live site has ever seen it. Whether it was withdrawn deliberately or commented out by accident is not known.

Those numbers are published here on the university operator's instruction, because the alternative — a university website with no route to campus security and none to its medical service — is worse. They are marked throughout as present in the captured source but not published, which is a different claim from "the university publishes this". If one of them turns out to be stale, that marking is what lets somebody work out why.

This is a demonstration site. Every page carries a banner saying so and asks search engines not to index it, and that is what makes publishing an unpublished internal number acceptable here. If this site's status ever changes, these rows are the first thing to re-check — before anything else on the page.

Complaints, welfare and statutory committees

The committees a student or member of staff can write to directly. Each publishes a committee mailbox rather than a person, which is the shape the rest of this directory is working towards.

Anti-Ragging Committee and Squad

Ragging complaints from students and staff. Ragging is a criminal offence under the UGC regulations. No telephone number is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published. The national ragging prevention page carries the heading "Contact Details of the Nodal Officers of Anti-Ragging Committee and Squad" and then gives no contact details for any nodal officer.

Email argc@mitwpu.edu.in

Internal Complaints Committee

Complaints of sexual harassment from students and employees. Chairperson: not yet published. The captured page names a Chairperson without naming who holds the post. No telephone number is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published.

Email icc@mitwpu.edu.in

Students' Grievance Redressal Committee

Student grievances under the UGC (Redressal of Grievances of Students) Regulations, 2023, and the route on to the Ombudsperson. Chairperson: not yet published. No telephone number is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published. A grievance unresolved by the committee is referred to the Ombudsperson. The university publishes no contact for the Ombudsperson.

Email grc@mitwpu.edu.in

SC/ST and Equal Opportunity Cell

Complaints and suggestions from students under the Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe provisions. Member Secretary: not yet published. No telephone number is published for this office, no room or building is published and no opening hours are published.

Email scstc@mitwpu.edu.in

No office in this part of the university is recorded yet.

Offices students, applicants and families reach directly

Admissions, examinations, fees, hostel and the day-to-day services of the university.

Applications, entrance examinations, admission counselling and document verification for every programme. Director - Admissions: Dr. Prakash Mainkar. Where it is: MIT-WPU, Kothrud Campus. Opening hours: Monday - Saturday : 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Buildings, grounds and campus maintenance. The only estates contact anywhere on the university's public site. Civil Engineer: not yet published. The same ground, covered elsewhere

Two other places on this site cover overlapping ground and are not repeated here.

  • Report a problem, request a service The same material arranged by what has gone wrong rather than by which office owns it. Read that page when you have a symptom and this one when you know the office.
  • People directory Six hundred and eight academic and research staff profiles. Post-holders below link into it where a profile exists.
  • Administrative Offices (as the university publishes it) The captured page this directory is built from: twelve offices, one email address each, no telephone numbers.
  • Contact (as the university publishes it) The captured contact page: a campus address, two admissions numbers and a WhatsApp line.

Offices this university publishes nothing about

The offices below are ones a university of this size normally has and which nothing in the capture mentions at all. They are not gaps in this page: they are questions for whoever knows the institution.

Each was looked for across roughly 1,200 captured routes and found nowhere.

  • Library / Knowledge Resource Centre — The university writes "Knowledge Resource Centre" in its own prose. No office, no contact, no opening hours.
  • Accessibility and disability support — No office, no coordinator, no statement of provision.
  • Counselling and mental-health service — The medical facilities page covers physical health only.
  • Ombudsperson — Named in the grievance procedure as the escalation from the grievance committee. No contact published.
  • Transport — No office for campus or shuttle transport.
  • Alumni relations — Named as one of CIAP's functions, with no separate route.
  • Research office / grants and sponsored projects — No administrative office for research funding, ethics or contracts.
  • Scholarships and financial aid — Scholarships are published as a scheme with no office behind them.
  • Lost property and campus reception — No front-desk or reception contact of any kind.
  • Data protection / information officer — No named contact for a privacy or right-to-information request.
  • Procurement and vendor relations — No route for a supplier.
  • Sports and physical education — Facilities are described; no office runs them.
