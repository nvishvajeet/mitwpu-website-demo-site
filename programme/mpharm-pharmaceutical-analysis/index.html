M.Pharm Pharmaceutical Analysis

Master the art of analyzing pharmaceutical substances with the M.Pharm Pharmaceutical Analysis program at MIT-WPU. Develop expertise in analytical techniques, quality control, method validation, and regulatory compliance. Our industry-oriented curriculum, advanced instrumentation, and experienced faculty ensure a comprehensive learning experience. Prepare for rewarding careers in pharmaceutical analysis, research and development, quality assurance, and regulatory affairs. Join MIT-WPU's M.Pharm Pharmaceutical Analysis program and become a sought-after professional in the field of pharmaceutical sciences. Apply now to enhance your analytical skills and contribute to ensuring the safety and efficacy of pharmaceutical products.

Eligibility

Minimum 55% aggregate marks in a B. Pharm. degree from an institution recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India or an equivalent examination from a government-approved university/ institution (Minimum 50% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). Additionally, Candidates must be registered with the State Pharmacy Council. Those who are not registered at the time of admission must submit the registration certificate within one month of securing admission.

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the GPAT 2026, 2025, or 2024 score and Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University according to the prescribed schedule. For admission under sponsored category, candidate must have a minimum of two years of full-time work experience in a registered firm, company, industry, educational/research institute, Government Department, or Government Autonomous Organization in the relevant field in which admission is sought. A Sponsorship Certificate (in the prescribed format) is mandatory for admission under this category

FAQs

  • What are the main subjects covered in the curriculum?

    Key subjects include modern pharmaceutical analytical techniques, advanced pharmaceutical analysis, pharmaceutical validation, food analysis, advanced instrumental analysis, bio-analytical techniques, quality control and assurance, and herbal and cosmetic analysis.

  • What practical training is provided?

    The programme features extensive laboratory work, hands-on training with advanced analytical instruments, and project-based learning to ensure practical skill development.

  • What career opportunities are available after graduation?

    Graduates can pursue roles in quality control, analytical research, regulatory affairs, pharmaceutical manufacturing, academia, and research organisations.

  • Is there industry exposure or internship support?

    Yes, the curriculum includes industry-oriented projects and MIT-WPU offers placement assistance and opportunities for internships with leading pharmaceutical companies.
