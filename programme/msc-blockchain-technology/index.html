Programme Overview

Blockchain Technology stands as a pivotal innovation, revolutionising how transactions are recorded and managed across various computer nodes in a decentralised ledger system. Its significance lies in ensuring secure, transparent, and immutable transactions across various sectors. To understand this intricate working of blockchain technology, an in-depth knowledge of this evolving field is imperative.

The M.Sc. Blockchain Technology programme at MIT-WPU is a comprehensive two-year full-time programme, designed to immerse students into varied case studies related to blockchain technologies, encouraging them to solve real-world problems and work on extensive datasets while learning core subjects.

The teaching methodology is structured to offer foundational knowledge and practical insights. The initial year covers fundamental subjects like the Introduction to Blockchain Ecosystem, Advanced Database Management System, Transaction Management using MIS, Research Methodology, and Databases. Students undergo training in Python and Advanced Database Management Systems while engaging in sessions focusing on peace and ethical behaviour.

FAQs

  • What is Blockchain Technology?

    Blockchain technology is a decentralised digital ledger that securely records transactions across a network of computers. Using cryptographic techniques, it creates linked blocks of data that are transparent and tamper-resistant. Beyond cryptocurrencies, blockchain is used in finance, supply chain, healthcare, and more for secure, efficient, and transparent record-keeping.

  • How does the curriculum of an M.Sc. in Blockchain Technology programme at MIT-WPU compare to international standards?

    The M.Sc.Blockchain Technology programme at MIT-WPU is designed to align with international standards, emphasising advanced topics of the industry. The curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications through labs, real-world projects, and industry-aligned certifications, ensuring graduates meet global competencies. Additionally, MIT-WPU’s focus on interdisciplinary learning, research opportunities, and collaborations with global tech leaders enhances students' readiness for global blockchain roles.

  • What makes MIT-WPU’s M.Sc. Blockchain Technology programme unique?

    MIT-WPU’s M.Sc. Blockchain Technology programme is designed to align with industry needs, offering exposure to the latest advancements in blockchain, cloud security, and AI integration, preparing students for the demands of Industry 4.0.

  • How does blockchain technology extend beyond cryptocurrencies?

    Blockchain technology is used in various sectors such as finance, healthcare, supply chain management, and digital identity, offering transparency, security, and efficiency in transactions.

The M.Sc. Blockchain Technology programme at MIT-WPU offers a curriculum, featuring core majors in Blockchain Technology and Applications, Blockchain and IoT Integration, and Cloud Platform Solutions. Designed to equip students with the latest skills, the programme covers critical areas of blockchain innovation, secure decentralised systems, and cross-application cloud solutions, positioning graduates at the forefront of this technology landscape.
