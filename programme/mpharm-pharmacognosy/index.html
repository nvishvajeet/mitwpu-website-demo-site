Programme Overview

The M.Pharm in Pharmacognosy programme at MIT-WPU focuses on the study of natural drugs and their various physical, chemical, microscopical, and biological properties. It also involves the search for novel drugs from natural sources. This programme trains students in the extraction and isolation of compounds from natural sources for medicinal purposes, and prepares them for careers in the pharmaceutical, herbal/ayurvedic, nutraceutical, and cosmeceutical industries. The increased demand for standardisation and analysis of herbal medications has created a need for trained pharmacognosy professionals, and this programme provides students with the opportunity to research the chemical ingredients found in nature and evaluate their potential for disease treatment.

Eligibility

Minimum 55% aggregate marks in a B. Pharm. degree from an institution recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India or an equivalent examination from a government-approved university/ institution (Minimum 50% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). Additionally, Candidates must be registered with the State Pharmacy Council. Those who are not registered at the time of admission must submit the registration certificate within one month of securing admission.

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the GPAT 2026, 2025, or 2024 score and Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University according to the prescribed schedule. For admission under sponsored category, candidate must have a minimum of two years of full-time work experience in a registered firm, company, industry, educational/research institute, Government Department, or Government Autonomous Organization in the relevant field in which admission is sought. A Sponsorship Certificate (in the prescribed format) is mandatory for admission under this category

FAQs

  • What is the focus of the M.Pharm Pharmacognosy programme at MIT-WPU?

    The programme centers on the study of natural drugs, including their physical, chemical, microscopical, and biological properties. It also covers the extraction, isolation, and evaluation of compounds from natural sources for medicinal purposes, aiming to discover novel drugs.

  • What are the key subjects and areas covered in the curriculum?

    The curriculum includes advanced pharmacognosy, extraction and isolation techniques, phytochemistry, quality control, drug discovery from natural sources, and research methodology. Students also undertake practical training and research projects.

  • What career opportunities are available after completing this programme?

    Graduates can pursue careers in the pharmaceutical, herbal/ayurvedic, nutraceutical, and cosmeceutical industries, as well as in research, quality control, regulatory affairs, and academia.

  • Are there any industry collaborations or internships?

    The programme includes opportunities for internships and industry exposure, helping students gain practical experience in relevant sectors.
