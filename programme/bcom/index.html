Programme Overview

The MIT-WPU B.Com (Hons.) programme has an extensive and application-oriented curriculum that equips you with qualifying abilities for various business and finance careers. Cost Accounting, Banking and Finance, and Marketing Management specialisations provide a strong foundation in core business disciplines.

In addition to the industry-centric content, the curriculum is enriched with case studies, expert sessions, and live projects, making it a highly application-oriented programme in sync with actual business practices.

Apart from theoretical curriculum, practical skills are honed through electives and a choice-based credit system that helps students to customise the studies as per career options. Industry experts, business leaders, and international academic immersion programmes all ensure you get a taste of global business trends.

  • How does MIT-WPU approach its teaching methodology?

    MIT-WPU learning approach incorporates case studies, practical hands-on projects, and expert sessions to equip students with the skills needed in the business world today. This aims to make graduates more employable and ready for success in real-world business environments.

  • What are the job prospects after completing the B.Com programme?

    Graduates may pursue careers in sectors such as accounting, investment banking, financial analysis, consulting, and more.

  • Is there any career guidance and placement assistance provided by MIT-WPU?

    Yes, MIT-WPU offers 100% internship placement assistance, career counselling, and guidance to help students prepare for leading industry organisations.

  • What is the difference between BBA and B.Com?

    The BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) is a programme designed to focus on management, entrepreneurship, and business operations, while the B.Com programme is centred around accounting, taxation, and finance, preparing students for roles in finance and accounting.
