Programme Overview

The Bachelor of Commerce Financial Analysis (Hons.) programme is designed to equip students with the essential skills and knowledge required to excel in the modern finance world. In collaboration with the CFA Institute, an institution from the USA known for its Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) qualification, this programme offers a specialised curriculum that aligns with key areas of finance.

Key focus areas include Financial Analysis, Corporate Finance, Portfolio Management, and Wealth Management, providing a solid foundation for those aspiring to build careers in finance. In addition to core subjects from the Commerce and Management domains, the programme incorporates elements of the CFA curriculum, offering students an edge in professional financial qualifications.

With a robust emphasis on Accounting & Finance, the Bachelor of Commerce (Financial Analysis) ensures students are well-prepared to meet the demands of the evolving financial landscape.

Major Tracks

  • Corporate Finance & Financial Reporting
  • Wealth Management & Investment Banking
  • Global Financial Systems & Compliance
  • Core CFA Curriculum & Exam Preparation

Programme Highlights

  • Certification: Earn the Investment Foundations Certificate by the CFA Institute, USA, along with on-campus training, strengthening students’ understanding of finance and investment concepts.
  • Strong Financial Foundation : B.Com provides comprehensive insights into accounting, economics, and business management, ensuring students understand key financial concepts.
  • Advanced Financial Expertise: CFA focuses on specialised areas such as investment analysis, portfolio management, and ethical practices, offering a deeper, global perspective on finance.
  • Enhanced Employability: The CFA designation is highly respected in the finance industry, giving graduates a competitive edge in the job market.
  • Career Advancement: The integrated programme equips students with both academic and professional skillsets, opening doors to higher-paying roles and faster career growth.
  • Preparation for CFA Qualification: Students are encouraged to pursue the full CFA qualification alongside or after their undergraduate degree, enhancing their career prospects.
  • Global and Local Financial Insight: The programme offers a balance of global investment strategies and an understanding of the Indian financial landscape, making students professionals.
  • In-Demand Financial Skills: Graduates are equipped with critical financial analysis, portfolio management, and corporate finance skills, preparing them for various roles in the finance sector.

FAQs

  • What is the B.Com Financial Analysis (Integrated with CFA) programme at MIT-WPU?

    This is a 3-year undergraduate degree that combines a strong foundation in commerce with advanced training in financial analysis and direct preparation for the CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) exams. The programme integrates the CFA Institute Investment Foundations Certificate and offers on-campus training for CFA Level 1 and Level 2 exams.

  • What makes this programme unique?
    • Integrated CFA curriculum and support for CFA Level 1 and 2 preparation
    • Strong academic foundation in finance, accounting, and business
    • On-campus training, expert sessions, and industry exposure
    • Focus on employability and global financial standards
  • What career opportunities are available after graduation?

    Graduates can pursue roles in financial analysis, investment banking, consulting, accounting, and related fields. The CFA integration enhances employability in top finance roles and global firms.

  • Does MIT-WPU provide placement assistance?

    Yes, the university offers 100% placement assistance, career counselling, and internship support to students.
