Programme Overview

This uniquely designed B.Des. (Interior and Space Design) programme at MIT-WPU will provide you with a strong foundation in the principles of interior design, while also embracing the latest trends in this ever-evolving field. The curriculum integrates residential, office, healthcare, retail, and event spaces, equipping you with skills in functional aesthetics and practical insight to create engaging, user-focused spaces. With a focus on sustainability, smart home technology, and environmental psychology, this programme trains you to design inclusive and accessible spaces that adapt to the changing needs of modern life.

The programme also exposes you to advanced technologies such as virtual reality and green building practices, enabling you to work with 3D modelling, digital rendering, and material selection. Applied learning through internships and industry collaborations ensures that you are job-ready upon graduation. The curriculum incorporates research-grounded design thinking, allowing you to understand the psychological and sensory impacts of space.

Selection Process

Admissions will be based purely on the merit of MIT-WPU CET 2026/ UCEED 2026/ NIFT 2026/ NATA 2026/ NID 2026 score, along with Personal Interaction (PI) and Portfolio Review, as per the prescribed schedule. A valid score in UCEED/ NATA/ NIFT/ NID may also be considered for the selection process; in such cases, the candidate must additionally appear for the MIT-WPU CET Personal Interaction (PI), Portfolio Review, and Drawing Test as per the schedule

Programme Highlights

  • Innovative Design Techniques : Study advanced design principles, embracing sustainable and smart home technologies, as well as using cutting-edge design software tools.
  • Theoretical Knowledge : Understanding concepts, principles, and methodologies that govern your design process.
  • Early Exposure with Industry : Opportunities for internships and co-design proposals with top design studios.
  • Domain & Disciplinary Integration : You will get an all-encompassing understanding of space design, with the curriculum combining architecture, environmental psychology and design technology.
  • Hands-On Projects : Get Your Hands DirtymdLearn Through Monthly Projects to Boost Your Creativity and Practical Sensibilities
  • Extracurriculars : Get involved with a design-related organization, as this can further develop your leadership and teamwork skills.
  • Emphasis on Global Design Challenges : Tackle concerns such as sustainability and the demand for human-focused environments.
  • Solid Industry-Academia Connect : Gain from internships and placements due to association with leading design professionals and organizations. Access to Alumni Network and Mentorship : Get guidance from a large network of industry experts and alumni.
  • Entrepreneurial Development : MIT-WPU Pune Technology Business Incubator (TBI) offers support for startups within the interior and space design sector to secure funding and mentorship.

Programme Outcomes

  • Use your understanding of space planning, material technology, user psychology and building services to inform design projects that fulfil user requirements.
  • Transform human experiences and behaviour into spaces that shape and adapt according to ever-changing user needs.
  • Manage multi-disciplinary projects, acting as a team member or lead.
  • Articulate with a range of stakeholders to document, deliver, and implement design solutions.
  • Design interiors that solve social, health, safety, cultural, and legislative challenges.
  • Apply principles of sustainable design, while considering the environmental and social impact of your work.
  • Show ethical and professional responsibilities within the design profession.
  • Focus on lifelong learning and continuous technological advancement in interior and space design.
  • Develop your entrepreneurial skills by learning about the inner workings of a business in the interior design field, so you can prepare to launch and run your own design firm.

FAQs

  • Which methods are applied to assess students during the programme?

    The students are assessed with portfolios, examinations, group projects and industry internships; practical and theoretical knowledge is paramount.

  • Although, what are the job prospects after a B.Des in Interior programme?

    On graduation, they can work as interior designers, space planners, lighting designers and visual merchandisers, as well as take up entrepreneurship within the arena of design.

  • Is there any guidance or help provided regarding placements?

    Yes, the institute has robust placement support, collaborating with top interior design companies and industry experts.

  • B.Des Interior Design at MIT-WPU: Why this Programme?

    It is this blend of creative and practical knowledge that will lead you to create functional and beautiful spaces with the B.Des Interior Design programme from MIT-WPU. You’ll also get up to date on trends such as sustainability and smart technology, along with real-world know-how via internships and projects. With guidance from expert faculty and industry connections, you’ll be prepared for a successful career in interior design.

  • What is the B.Des Interior Design Curriculum Synopsis?
    • Green Design : Understand how to design energy-efficient and eco-friendly spaces
    • Lighting Design : Learn how to implement lighting that enhances the mood and utility of spaces.
    • Materials and Finishes : Descriptive use of diverse materials to make visual continuity and durability for spaces.
    • Space planning : Learn how to effectively plan and stage the various spaces inside a building.
    • Essential Interior Design Skills : Understand the key skills essential for all interior designers.
