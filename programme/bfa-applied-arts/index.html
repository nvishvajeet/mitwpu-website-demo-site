Programme Overview

Explore a multi-dimensional career as a visual artist with a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Applied Arts at MIT-WPU. This course places a strong emphasis on hands-on experience, innovative problem-solving, and the practical application of knowledge, preparing you for a dynamic career in fields such as advertising, branding, graphic design, e-learning, and more. The curriculum covers topics, including graphic design, typography, illustration, packaging design, and photography, alongside both theoretical and practical training. Additionally, students have the opportunity to gain extra certifications through MOOCs, enhancing their industry-readiness.

The programme places significant emphasis on real-world experience, exposing you to current marketplace trends through internships, live projects, and industry visits. These opportunities are designed to prepare you for the ever-evolving creative field. Special modules like Peace Studies promote development and equip you with essential skills, such as teamwork, communication, and leadership, which are vital for success in any profession.

The BFA in Applied Arts programme trains graduates to craft impactful marketing campaigns, produce original content, and build compelling brand identities. With a skill set combining both creative and technical expertise, you will graduate ready to thrive as an innovative practitioner in a creative agency or through freelance projects, contributing to the rapidly growing applied arts and media industries.

Eligibility

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in Class 10+2/ HSC or its equivalent examination in any stream with English subject (Minimum 45% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admissions will be based purely on the merit of MIT-WPU CET 2026 Personal Interaction (PI), Portfolio Review, and Drawing Test by a Jury. The Portfolio Review and Drawing Test will be exempted if the candidate has a valid score in UCEED/PERA/MAH-CET (Applied Arts); however, the candidate must additionally appear for the MIT-WPU CET Personal Interaction (PI) as per the schedule.

Programme Highlights

  • Curriculum: Covers an industry-relevant curriculum. It integrates the latest tools and techniques in visual arts, graphic design, and advertising.
  • Practical Exposure : A strong focus on essays, domain tours and active projects to equip students for the workforce
  • Industry Inclusive : Gain knowledge from real-world experience through faculty members with both academic and industry exposure.
  • Specialisation in Various Tracks : Offers specialisation electives on insight-based topics like illustration, typography, and photography, based on your career choices.
  • Internships & Placement Support : With a compulsory six-month internship, students gain exposure to real-world problems, and the university extends 100% placement support.
  • Global Exposure: experience in rural, national, and international settings to widen your global horizon and practical knowledge
  • Growth: Taught along with modules of Peace Studies, and co-curricular activities.

Programme Outcomes

  • Use fundamental principles of graphic design, typography, and illustrative work for practical applications.
  • Incorporating cultural, social and environmental contexts; User-centric design solutions
  • Gain a clear understanding of design software tools and techniques, and apply this knowledge in your projects.
  • Work collaboratively as a creative and professional team to solve complex design problems.
  • Build a portfolio of design projects and solutions to showcase to the industry.
  • Be an entrepreneur by embracing design thinking in business models and creating new visual design projects.
  • Adhere to ethical design paradigms that recognize solutions that consider sustainability and world issues.

FAQs

  • What emphasis is placed on real-world ability and background in this course?

    Internships, live projects, and industry visits ensure that students are industry-ready as the programme focuses a great deal on exposure to the industry.

  • Can I find out about internships specifically or ways to work with companies I can work with?

    Yes, the course has an inbuilt mandatory internship for all students and 100% placement assistance with tie-ups with design agencies and brands.

  • What does it teach its students in terms of utilising software and tools?

    Practical workshops and assignments allow students to use industry-standard software, such as Adobe Suite, CorelDRAW, and AutoCAD.

  • What training and expertise does the BFA Applied Arts programme faculty bring?

    The faculty is led by professionals working in the industry, therefore, students not only gain theoretical knowledge but also have practical insights into the field.

  • Why BFA Applied Arts at MIT-WPU?
    • Comprehensive Learning : The BFA Applied Arts at MIT-WPU is a comprehensive degree that covers creativity and hands-on training with an emphasis on learning industry-relevant skill sets.
    • Industry Exposure : Moreover, you get internships, live projects, industry visits, etc. which add experience, gain insights into professional challenges.
    • Experienced Faculty : Discover how experts with academic and industry experience guide you through the creative and technical aspects of the arts.
    • Skill Development : Get skills in fields such as graphic design, photography, illustration and others, benefiting from the most sophisticated tools and sources.
    • Growth: The addition of Peace Studies and soft skills training ensures is a complete package for your body and mind.
  • what is the BFA Applied Arts Curriculum?
    • Graphic Design : Understand how to make high-quality visual designs for posters, branding, and online.
    • Illustration : Learn to create illustrations for books, media, and creative projects.
    • Animation : To create exciting animated visuals, learn the fundamentals of animation!
    • Photography : Learn photography basics and take steps to visual storytelling with images.
    • Typography : Translate your words into design with effective fonts and typefaces
