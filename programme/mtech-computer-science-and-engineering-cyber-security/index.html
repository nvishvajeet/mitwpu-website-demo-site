Programme Overview

The M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering(Cyber Security) at MIT-WPU offers a comprehensive programme designed to build expertise in cybersecurity through a blend of advanced theory and practical application.

Students explore critical areas such as network management, advanced cryptography, network programming, digital forensic analysis, and wireless security, mastering the techniques needed to protect digital ecosystems.

The programme empowers students to identify, mitigate, and respond to cybersecurity threats effectively, by putting a strong emphasis on proactive monitoring and robust defence strategies. Hands-on experience is guided by industry experts, allowing our students to implement cybersecurity techniques in real-world scenarios.

These promote both data protection and ethical conduct in managing sensitive information. Additionally, the M.Tech in Computer Science Cyber Security at MIT-WPU provides extensive internship and placement assistance across various cybersecurity roles, providing our graduates with dynamic and impactful careers in the cyber domain.

Eligibility

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in a 4- year graduation degree in the relevant Engineering branch* from a UGCapproved institution/ university or its equivalent (Minimum 45% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the merit of the MIT-WPU CET 2026 Entrance Examination, along with a Personal Interaction (PI) score conducted by MIT-WPU as per the prescribed schedule.

A valid score in GATE score (2026/ 2025/ 2024), PERA-CET 2026 will also be considered for the selection process; in such cases, the candidate must additionally appear for the Personal Interaction (PI) Score conducted by MIT-WPU as per the prescribed schedule.

For admission under sponsored category, candidate must have a minimum of two years of full-time work experience in a registered firm, company, industry, educational/ research institute, Government Department, or Government Autonomous Organization in the relevant field in which admission is sought. A Sponsorship Certificate (in the prescribed format) is mandatory for admission under this category.

* For more information regarding programme-wise branch eligibility, please refer to the M.Tech. rules published on the official website.

Career Prospects

  • What are the core subjects typically covered in an M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity) programme at MIT-WPU?

    The M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering Cyber Security at MIT-WPU delves into the study of network management, advanced cryptography, network programming, digital forensic analysis, and wireless security.

  • How does the curriculum of an M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity) programme at MIT-WPU compare to international standards?

    The M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity) at MIT-WPU is designed to align with international standards, emphasising advanced topics like cryptography, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and network security. The curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications through labs, real-world projects, and industry-aligned certifications, ensuring graduates meet global competencies. Additionally, MIT-WPU’s focus on interdisciplinary learning, research opportunities, and collaborations with global tech leaders enhances students' readiness for international cybersecurity roles.

  • What are the typical eligibility criteria for admission to an M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity) programme?

    Minimum 50% aggregate score in graduation (4 years) of relevant Engineering Branch from UGC approved University or its equivalent (at least 45% marks, in case of Reserved Class category candidate belonging to Maharashtra State only) AND GATE Qualified (Obtained a positive score in GATE 2025 / 2024 / 2023) /MIT-WPU CET 2025 /PERA CET 2025 OR Sponsored Candidate (Need 2 years of work experience after graduation, in field related to graduation).

  • What are the entrance exams commonly accepted for admission to M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity) programmes. (e.g., GATE, CAT)?

    MIT-WPU CET, PERA CET, or GATE are the exams commonly accepted for admission to M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity).

  • What are the major career paths available after completing an M.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Cybersecurity)?

    The major career paths after completing an M. Tech in Computer Science Cybersecurity:

    • Cybersecurity Specialist
    • Data Scientist
    • AI/ML Engineer
    • Software Development Engineer
    • Government Jobs
    • Teaching and Academia
    • Research and Development
    • IT Management and Consultancy
