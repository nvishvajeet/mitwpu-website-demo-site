Programme Overview

MIT-WPU's B.Tech (2nd Year Lateral Entry) programme provides diploma holders a streamlined programme to complete their engineering degree in just three years. This specialised pathway builds upon your existing practical foundation while introducing advanced theoretical concepts across engineering disciplines. By directly entering the second year of B.Tech studies, you save valuable time while gaining comprehensive technical expertise. The programme seamlessly bridges the gap between diploma and degree-level engineering education, positioning you for earlier entry into competitive job markets.

The curriculum blends advanced theoretical knowledge with your practical diploma experience, creating well-rounded engineering professionals. You'll benefit from MIT-WPU's cutting-edge facilities, expert faculty mentorship, and extensive industry partnerships that enhance your career prospects through integrated job placement support.

Eligibility

A minimum of 60% marks in a Diploma in Engineering and Technology in the relevant branch, obtained in the academic years 2025-26, 2024-25, or 2023-24, from an AICTE-approved or Central/State Government approved Institution/Board or its equivalent. (Minimum 55% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State)

Selection Process

Admission is based on the candidate’s Diploma scores and a 500-word Statement of Purpose (SOP).

FAQs

  • What are career opportunities After B.Tech Lateral Entry?

    Upon completing your B.Tech through lateral entry, you'll be qualified for diverse roles across multiple industries. Career paths include software development, mechanical engineering, electronics engineering, civil engineering, data science, and project management. MIT-WPU's robust industry partnerships ensure strong placement opportunities in top companies across IT, manufacturing, automotive, electronics, and consulting sectors.

  • What is the meaning of industry-relevant learning?

    MIT-WPU's B.Tech lateral entry programme prioritises practical industry preparation through internships, live projects, and workshops led by industry professionals. These experiences provide real-world exposure and develop employability skills that give graduates a competitive edge in the job market.

  • Is there job placement assistance provided by the university?

    Yes, MIT-WPU provides 100% placement assistance through its Center of Industry-Academia Partnerships. Tata Technologies, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, etc. and many leading companies have collaborated/partnerships with leading companies in the relevant sectors. The placement centre would help you to build your resume, prepare for interviews, and introduce you to potential employers, hence facilitating your transition from academia to industry.

  • What is lateral entry in B.Tech? What are the Benefits of Choosing Lateral Entry in B.Tech at MIT-WPU?

    The B.Tech Lateral Entry programme enables diploma holders to enter directly into the second year of the B.Tech programme. Key benefits include:

    • Earning a prestigious engineering degree in just 3 years
    • Seamless transition from diploma to complete B.Tech qualification
    • Enhanced employment prospects with a full B.Tech degree
    • Cost savings by completing your education one year faster
    • Respected university credentials that open numerous career pathways
