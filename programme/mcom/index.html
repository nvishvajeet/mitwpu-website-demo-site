Programme Overview

The Master of Commerce is a Post-Graduate Commerce programme in collaboration with CIMA, i.e. the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants. It emphasises business-related concepts, logical reasoning, critical thinking, and analysis.

The intended effect of the programme outline is to address the evolving demand for quantitative and analytical problem-solving abilities. With 'Research' as a recently introduced specialisation to equip students for both industry and academic requirements, the programme is research-driven.

The Department provides students with many opportunities to develop their research skills by planning and encouraging them to take part in and present research papers at various seminars, conclaves, symposiums, workshops, and conferences.

The primary research domains include strategy, business policy, small and medium-sized businesses, and accounting. Studies in industry, stock trading, public finance, and policy. International trade and finance, entrepreneurship and business models, marketing, and others to choose from.

In addition to obtaining a M.Com. degree, students might decide to enrol in CIMA. A CIMA Advanced Diploma in Management Accounting and a Master of Commerce Degree from MIT-WPU will be granted to the student upon completing the CIMA Management.

Eligibility

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in 3/4-year graduation degree in Commerce/ Management from a UGC-approved institution/university or its equivalent (minimum 45% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University according to the prescribed schedule

Programme Highlights

  • Globally Recognised Certification : Earn the Investment Foundations Certificate from the CFA Institute, USA, building a strong foundation in investment concepts and financial analysis.
  • Flexible Learning : A hybrid learning format designed for working professionals.
  • Research-Based Learning : Teaching methods grounded in research and case studies.
  • Practical Application : A Pedagogy that is practical and application-oriented.
  • Industry Insights : Visits to the industry, guest lectures, seminars, and workshops led by eminent corporate leaders from the Big Four.
  • Tailored Learning : A customizable curriculum with a wide range of elective subjects.
  • Industry-Relevant Curriculum : Regularly updated courses to stay current with industry trends.
  • Real-World Experience : Live industry projects to simulate real-world challenges.
  • Enhanced Employability : Additional certifications and skill-development courses to improve employability
  • Career-Focused Learning : Focus on skill development and competency development through value-added courses like Forensic Accounting, Advanced MS Excel, Financial Modeling, Six Sigma, and so on.
  • Comprehensive Support : 100% placement assistance for internships.

Programme Outcomes

  • Develop advanced knowledge in accounting, finance, and commerce to analyse and solve complex business problems.
  • Acquire professional skills for careers in banking, financial services, taxation, auditing, and corporate sectors.
  • Demonstrate effective communication, teamwork, and leadership abilities essential for managerial roles.
  • Apply ethical principles and social responsibility in business decision-making and professional conduct.
  • Utilise research and analytical skills to contribute to academic, industry, or entrepreneurial initiatives.
  • Adapt to dynamic business environments through lifelong learning and upskilling in emerging areas of commerce and management.

FAQs

  • What career opportunities are available after completing M.Com at MIT-WPU?

    Graduates can pursue roles such as Budget Analyst, Investment Banker, Research Analyst, Market and Policy Researcher, and other positions in finance, banking, and corporate sectors.

  • Is there any Scholarship for the M.Com. Programme?

    Yes, MIT-WPU provides 2000+ scholarships based on merit and entrance exam performance, which can significantly reduce the tuition fees for eligible students.

  • Are there opportunities for industry exposure and internships?

    Yes, the curriculum includes practical exposure and opportunities for internships to enhance employability and professional skills.

  • What is the teaching methodology at MIT-WPU?

    The programme combines updated curriculum, practical training, industry exposure, and value-based education to ensure holistic development.
