Programme Overview

The B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Electronics and Communication Engineering - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at MIT-WPU is a six-year programme that starts with the basics before advancing into specialised fields such as computer vision, deep learning, augmented reality (AR), and virtual reality (VR). It combines fundamental electronics with AI and machine learning and prepares you to enter cutting-edge fields like robotics, biomedical engineering, and automotive electronics.

Early exposure to practical learning equips you to design and develop intelligent systems for healthcare, business automation, and other sectors. Learn AI and ML with mentoring from expert instructors while acquiring both knowledge and practical skills. Such early exposure gives you a competitive advantage to forge a successful career in contemporary tech domains.

Major Tracks

  • Edge Intelligence
  • AI in Healthcare
  • AI Computing Platform
  • Deep Learning Architectures
  • Augmented and Virtual Reality

Eligibility

Minimum 60% aggregate marks in the Class 10/ SSC examination from a government-approved board or its equivalent, with Science and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. (Minimum 55% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the MIT-WPU CET 2026 Entrance Examination and the Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University as per the prescribed schedule.

Programme Highlights

  • Advanced Facilities : The programme provides modern laboratories with advanced tools and technologies available in the areas of AI, ML, IoT, and Robotics.
  • Industry Partnerships : MIT-WPU has strong collaborations with top-notch industries like Tata Motors, Chevrolet Cars, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Kirloskar Pneumatic Co. Ltd., Cognizant, Infosys, etc., which assist in providing students with on-the-job training and live projects.
  • Industry Internships : Work with various companies, such as Tata Motors, Sandvik Asia, John Deere, and CUMMINS India Ltd. These internships are great for practising your skills in real-world situations.
  • MOOCs & Interdisciplinary Courses : The curriculum includes Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) and interdisciplinary courses that enhance study related to business management, communication, and entrepreneurship.
  • Learn From Industry Experts : These courses are delivered by industry experts or leaders, keeping you updated on industry trends and future innovations.
  • Immersion Programmes : These opportunities expose you to a broader aspect of the industry from other parts of Bharat.

FAQs

  • Will I need to take the entrance exam to get admission?

    Yes, you must take the MIT-WPU CET Entrance Examination 2026, followed by Personal Interaction (PI) to get admission to the Integrated B.Tech in Electronics and Communication with AI and ML.

  • What are future opportunities after completing Diploma B.Tech in ECE?

    Graduates can work in robotics, automation, automotive electronics, data science, machine learning and others, and major job titles include machine learning engineer, AI research scientist and software architect.

  • What are the post-programme higher education or research opportunities?

    Yes, you can go for an M.Tech, MS or PhD in specialisation like AI, ML and computer vision after the completion of the programme or join research organisations that work on new technologies.

  • What is the campus life and facilities available at MIT-WPU?

    MIT World Peace University (MIT-WPU) is situated in Pune, India, they give students the opportunity to experience a broad range of campus life and amenities. These include the following :

    • Classrooms : Over 350 smart classrooms with projectors and smartboards.
    • Laboratories : 140+ state-of-the-art laboratories for research, experimentation, and invention, outfitted with the latest technology
    • The campus includes facilities for gymnasiums, dance studios, basketball courts, and common spaces.
    • Sports complex : Volleyball courts, badminton courts, basketball courts, cricket and football fields and other sports facilities whether it is an open area or closed
    • A gym' or 'fitness centre', having up-to-the-minute workout gear.
    • Aside from vending machines and cafeterias, there are other options to help you out with healthy meals.
    • Around-the-clock WiFi and a sprinkling of amenities
    • Professional surveillance every hour of the day using high-quality closed-circuit television cameras
    • A relaxing and revitalizing atmosphere that is perfect for meditation
    • Lawns dedicated to Yoga.
    • Services on campus for banking
    • It has an open-air amphitheatre outside.
    • It is a business incubator (non-profit incubation facility) to develop new ideas into products in a state-of-the-art facility.

    MIT-WPU has several other programmes and services as well :

    • Initiatives on social service

  • Internships Or Industry Collaborations: What are the Options?

    Yes, MIT-WPU offers extensive industry projects and internship opportunities with industry giants like Tata Motors, Volkswagen, and Mercedes-Benz.

  • Reasons to Choose Diploma B.Tech in Electronics and Communication

    MIT-WPU's Diploma B.Tech in Electronics and Communication: Good engineering directly after class 10 It integrates elementary academic learning in the basics of electronics, communication and advanced emerging technology areas like AI, Robotics and IoT. It is designed to help you build the foundation that is necessary to get practical knowledge of fields such as telecommunications, electronics, and automation. Equipped with experienced faculty, hands-on projects, and industry exposure, you will be a professional ready to embark on a successful career in this dynamic field.
