Programme Overview

The hospitality industry is experiencing robust growth, leading to an increasing demand for skilled professionals. The BBA in Hospitality and Hotel Administration at MIT-WPU equips students with a comprehensive understanding of the sector, covering all essential aspects of hospitality management.

This meticulously designed programme, developed by industry experts, focuses on critical areas such as food and beverage management, front office operations, accommodation management, personnel management, and financial accounting, aligning with industry standards.

The curriculum encompasses vital subjects including consumer behavior, sales strategies, marketing techniques, and management processes relevant to the hospitality sector. Students will acquire an education that includes insights into entertainment, catering, and culinary arts.

The programme emphasises practical training through national and international internships while also fostering academic knowledge. It encourages entrepreneurial initiatives and nurtures students' inherent talents. Regular engagement with industry professionals ensures that students stay informed about current trends, enhancing their employability upon graduation.

  • How does the programme at MIT-WPU incorporate practical training and internships?

    The BBA in Hospitality and Hotel Administration programme at MIT-WPU emphasises practical training through a structured internship component. Students engage in a 4 to 6-week internship during their studies, which provides them with hands-on experience in real-world hospitality settings. This practical exposure is crucial for understanding industry operations and enhancing employability

  • What are the key areas of study in the BBA in Hospitality and Hotel Administration at MIT-WPU?

    The curriculum covers several essential areas within hospitality and hotel administration, including:

    • Food and Beverage Management
    • Front Office Operations
    • Accommodation Management
    • Personnel Management
    • Financial and Hotel Accounting
    • Consumer Behavior and Marketing Strategies
    • Students also develop skills in leadership, communication, problem-solving, and sustainability practices relevant to the hospitality industry
  • Does MIT-WPU provide placement assistance?

    Yes, MIT-WPU has a dedicated Industry-Academia Center Partnerships to assist students in obtaining job opportunities in organisations through structured assessments, training, and grooming activities. They also claim 100% training and assistance with placement.

  • What kind of companies does MIT-WPU have tie-ups with for placements and internships?

    MIT-WPU has strong industry connections with top hotel brands in India and around the world, including Taj Hotels, The Oberoi Group, ITC Hotels, Marriott Hotels, Blue Diamond (IHCL SeleQtions), Courtyard by Marriott, Novotel Goa, Acacia Hotels & Villas Goa, and more.

Eligibility

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in Class 10+2/ HSC or its equivalent examination in any stream with English subject (Minimum 45% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). OR Minimum 60% aggregate marks in any 3 years Engineering Diploma from State Government approved Institution/ board.

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by MIT-WPU as per the prescribed schedule. During the Personal Interaction assessment is based on Communication skills, Analytical skills, General Awareness and Personality Traits.

Programme Outcomes

  • Comprehensive Understanding: Understand hospitality fundamentals and apply this knowledge across various sectors of the industry
  • Skill Development: Develop professional skills to create and evaluate hospitality operations, seizing opportunities as they arise
  • Efficiency Enhancement: Use their knowledge to improve operational efficiency in the hospitality and tourism industry
  • Develop strong leadership skills and effective communication abilities essential for managerial roles in the hospitality sector.
  • Cultivate a global mindset through exposure to international hospitality standards, practices, and cross-cultural experiences.
  • Enhance critical thinking and problem-solving abilities to address challenges in real-world hospitality scenarios.
  • Attain proficiency in managing diverse hotel functions, including food and beverage operations, housekeeping, and event management.
  • Embrace ethical business practices and understand the importance of sustainability in the context of hospitality and tourism.
  • Cultivate teamwork and interpersonal skills to collaborate effectively with diverse teams in the dynamic hospitality environment.
  • Adapt to evolving industry trends and foster an innovative mindset to contribute to the continuous improvement of hospitality services.
