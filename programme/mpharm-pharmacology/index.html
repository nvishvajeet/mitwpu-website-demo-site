M.Pharm Pharmacology

The M.Pharm Pharmacology programme at MIT-WPU covers pharmacological research, drug development, toxicology, and clinical pharmacology, supported by laboratories and experienced faculty. It prepares students for careers in pharmaceutical research, academia, clinical trials, and regulatory affairs.

Programme Overview

The M.Pharm Pharmacology programme at MIT-WPU provides a comprehensive study of pharmaceutical analytical techniques, advanced pharmacology, pharmacological and toxicological screening methods, cellular and molecular pharmacology, experimental pharmacology, clinical research and pharmacovigilance, drug discovery, research methodology, and biostatistics. Upon completing this programme, students will be well-prepared for career opportunities in the field of pharmacology, including positions in clinical research organisations, biomedical companies, IT companies specialising in pharmacovigilance, the pharmaceutical industry, private and government R&D laboratories, contract research organisations, and companies involved in the manufacturing of pharmaceutical and biological products.

Major Tracks

  • Advanced Pharmacology
  • Pharmacological and Toxicological Screening Methods
  • Principles of drug discovery
  • Clinical research and pharmacovigilance

Eligibility

Minimum 55% aggregate marks in a B. Pharm. degree from an institution recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India or an equivalent examination from a government-approved university/ institution (Minimum 50% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). Additionally, Candidates must be registered with the State Pharmacy Council. Those who are not registered at the time of admission must submit the registration certificate within one month of securing admission.

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the GPAT 2026, 2025, or 2024 score and Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University according to the prescribed schedule. For admission under sponsored category, candidate must have a minimum of two years of full-time work experience in a registered firm, company, industry, educational/research institute, Government Department, or Government Autonomous Organization in the relevant field in which admission is sought. A Sponsorship Certificate (in the prescribed format) is mandatory for admission under this category

FAQs

  • What is the duration of the M.Pharm in Pharmacology programme?

    It's a 2-year full-time postgraduate degree, structured into four semesters

  • What are the core areas covered in this programme?

    The curriculum includes modern pharmaceutical analytical techniques, advanced pharmacology, toxicological screening, cellular & molecular pharmacology, experimental pharmacology, clinical research & pharmacovigilance, drug discovery, research methodology, and biostatistics .

  • Are there hands-on research and training opportunities?

    Absolutely! The programme includes :

    • Experimental and practical lab work
    • Journal clubs and seminar assignments
  • What career paths are available after graduation?

    Graduates can enter industry and research roles such as:

    • Clinical research organizations
    • Biomedical/biopharma companies
    • Pharmacovigilance & drug safety
    • Pharma R&D (public/private labs)
    • Drug discovery firms
    • Teaching positions and governmental roles
