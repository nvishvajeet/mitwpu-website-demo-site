Programme Overview

MIT-WPU's B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Computer Science and Engineering offers an educational pathway for students who have completed their 10th standard education. This six-year comprehensive programme bridges secondary education with advanced engineering studies, creating a structured transition from school to university environment.

Students receive thorough training in core computer science domains including software development, data science, and cybersecurity, along with proficiency in essential programming languages such as C, C++, Java, and Python. This contemporary educational approach develops not only technical expertise but also cultivates crucial critical thinking, problem-solving, and research capabilities essential for thriving in today's dynamic technological landscape.

The programme provides students with early exposure to technologies including artificial intelligence, machine learning, and network security. The curriculum balances conceptual understanding with practical application, enhanced through industry collaborations, live internships, research opportunities, and real-world challenges

Major Tracks

  • Data Science Software Design and Development
  • Computer Vision and Multimedia Technologies
  • Cybersecurity and Information Security Systems
  • Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems
  • Cloud Computing and Distributed Infrastructure

Eligibility

Minimum 60% aggregate marks in the Class 10/ SSC examination from a government-approved board or its equivalent, with Science and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. (Minimum 55% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the MIT-WPU CET 2026 Entrance Examination and the Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University as per the prescribed schedule.

FAQs

  • How long is the B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Computer Science and Engineering course?

    The B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Computer Science and Engineering course is a six-year course at MIT-WPU, covering all the information from high school to B.Tech. The first two years will consist primarily of the foundational subjects: maths, science, as well as introductory programming. In the third year, you will have advanced courses in software development, data science, cyber security, and others. Such an integrated approach gives you a jumpstart in your engineering career, so by the time you complete your degree, you are ready to face industry challenges.

  • Does the programme offer any specialisations or concentrations?

    Yes, the programme does have various specialisations and tracks available from the third year onwards. These are Data Science, Cyber Security, Computer Vision, and Software Development. Each track is designed to teach you the latest trends in technology and industry. This will help you learn skills like machine learning, big data analytics, network security, and even cryptography, which can be useful in multiple career paths that branch out from the tech industry.

  • Is there a focus on practical skills and hands-on learning?

    Definitely! The B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Computer Science and Engineering course focuses heavily on practical skills and hands-on learning. You will gain in-depth theoretical knowledge in class, combined with laboratories and tools designed to turn that theory into practice by applying it to real-world problems. The programme implements industry-oriented projects and internships, along with workshops on innovations such as Python, IoT ecosystems, blockchain, and cyber security. These ensure you gain hands-on experience and equip you with practical skills for your career thereafter.

  • Does the programme provide any career guidance or placement assistance?

    Yes, along with 100% internship assistance, the university also has a dedicated Centre for Industry-Academia Partnerships (CIAP), which focuses on both internships and full-time job opportunities with top organisations like Tata Technologies, Capgemini, Volkswagen, Infosys, Cognizant, and more. The Career Services Centre provides guidance, resume polishing workshops, mock interviews, and placement drives, ensuring you are well-prepared for the recruitment process and ready to secure a job at top tech companies.

  • Why pursue the B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Computer Science and Engineering from MIT-WPU?
    • Education: It is a combination of basic learning with professional acquisition, which you gain through an aspiring education.
    • Industry-Relevant Skills : You will learn the most recent technologies and skills essential for future tech jobs.
    • Professional Teachers : Our professional trainers guide you through both theoretical and practical knowledge.
    • Creative Resources : Access to labs and tools that encourage creativity and allow you to work on real-life projects.
