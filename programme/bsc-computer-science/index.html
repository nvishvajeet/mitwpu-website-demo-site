Programme Overview

The B.Sc. Computer Science course at MIT-WPU is a full-time study with an emphasis on programming, databases, web development, and emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, cybersecurity, etc. The curriculum focuses heavily on practical application and prepares students to tackle real-world issues and drive innovation in the tech sector. The programme starts with the principles of computer science and advances into specialisations such as cloud computing, data science, network security, and blockchain technology.

The programme also offers practical experience through real-world projects and industry partnerships and prepares students for careers in software development, cybersecurity, and data analysis. The programme also offers international exposure with partners such as the University of Wisconsin, providing opportunities for exchange programmes and joint research.

Eligibility

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in Class 10+2/ HSC or its equivalent examination in science stream with Mathematics, & Statistics subject (Minimum 45% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). OR Minimum 55% aggregate marks in any 3 years Engineering Diploma from State Government approved Institution/ board.

  • Why choose B.Sc. Computer Science at MIT-WPU?

    The B.Sc. Computer Science syllabus includes a plethora of programming languages, web development, and databases, along with new technologies like AI, ML, and Cybersecurity. The programme equips students with all job prospects related to computer science through a practical approach towards critical thinking, problem-solving, and analysis.

    You can specialise your education in fields like Cloud Computing, Data Science, and Cybersecurity, so the courses can cater specifically to your career. The courses offered are comprehensive and lead professionals to success, including optional subjects such as Intellectual Property Rights, Data Mining, and Blockchain Technology. Experiential learning is a big component of the programme, and students work on real-world media and technology projects through internships to get ready for the competitive tech industry.

  • Overview of the Core Curriculum for B.Sc. Computer Science at MIT-WPU
    • Programming Fundamentals : Basics of computer languages (C, Python, Java), which is a foundation in software development.
    • Design and Analysis of Algorithms : Learn about the efficient organisation and processing of data to solve computational problems.
    • Database Management Systems : Learn methods of structuring, handling, and querying data with databases.
    • Web Technologies : Learn how to build interactive websites and web applications with modern tools and frameworks.
    • Networking and Security : Learn about computer networks, protocols, and security measures to defend data and systems.
