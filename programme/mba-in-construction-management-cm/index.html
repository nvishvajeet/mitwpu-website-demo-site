Programme Overview

The MBA Construction Management is a specialized postgraduate programme focused on developing managerial and leadership skills for the construction and infrastructure sector with a strong emphasis on digital construction. The programme integrates management principles with modern digital technologies such as Building Information Modelling (BIM) and project planning tools to enhance project planning, execution, and control. Through an industry- oriented and practice-driven approach, graduates are prepared for leadership roles in construction, infrastructure, and project management organizations.

Scholarship

Based on student merit, MIT-WPU offers multiple scholarships to promote inclusion and academic excellence.

These are stated as below,

i. Dr. Vishwanath Karad Scholarship This scholarship provides a 100% fee waiver for the entire duration of the Programme and is applicable for 5% of the total students’ intake. (Subject to the fulfilment of conditions mentioned under the respective policy).

ii. MIT-WPU Merit Scholarship I These scholarships are provided at the rate 50% fee waiver in total academic fee for the entire duration of the Programme up to 10% of the total students’ intake. (Subject to the fulfilment of conditions mentioned under the respective policy).

iii. MIT-WPU Merit Scholarship II These scholarships are provided at the rate of 25% fee waiver in total academic fee for the entire duration of the Programme up to 12% of the total students’ intake. (Subject to the fulfilment of conditions mentioned under the respective policy).

iv. Scholarship to Elite Sportsman These scholarships are awarded to deserving sports persons based on their participation/winning the prizes at Nationally/Internationally recognized sports competitions/events.

MIT-WPU Special Scholarship (First Cum First Serve Basis) : The first 10 admitted girl students and the first 10 admitted boy students who have secured a minimum of First Class in their 10th, 12th, and Graduation examinations shall be eligible for a 50% tuition fee waiver. In cases where a student is eligible for both the above tuition fee waiver and any MIT-WPU merit based scholarship, only the scholarship offering the higher financial benefit shall be awarded. The award of the above tuition fee waivers shall be strictly on a first-come, first-served basis.

Terms & Conditions Apply:

  • All Scholarships are awarded on a First Come First Serve basis. All Scholarships are awarded as fee adjustments.
  • To continue the scholarship for the entire duration of the programme, a minimum level of the academic score has to be maintained at an 8 CGPA across all semesters, attendance is to be maintained at a minimum of 80 percent, with no live backlogs in any subject/programme and no semester break, and there should be no disciplinary action against the student.

For more detailed information visit our website: https://mitwpu.edu.in/scholarships

Eligibility

Minimum 55% aggregate marks in a graduation degree in Civil Engineering/ Construction Engineering/ Infrastructure Engineering/ Environmental Engineering/ Architecture or Allied Branches* (Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Production Engineering) from a UGC-approved institution/ university or its equivalent (Minimum 50% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State). AND A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Class 10 (SSC) and Class 12 (HSC)/ 3-year Diploma examinations (post SSC), with English as a compulsory subject.

Selection Process

Candidates will also be evaluated through a 50-mark Personal Interaction (PI) score conducted by MIT-WPU as per the prescribed schedule, along with consideration of their past academic record and work experience. AND Admission will be based on the merit of a valid score in CAT 2025, XAT 2026, NMAT 2025, GMAT, CMAT 2026, MAH-CET 2026, PERA 2026 or MAT (September 2025 onwards) with valid (non-zero) national-level entrance score.

Note : Working professionals will get higher weightage in the selection process.

Curriculum Philosophy

The curriculum is industry-oriented, outcome-based and research-enriched. It includes:

  • Digital Construction & BIM
  • Project Controls: Cost, Schedule, Risk
  • Contracts, Claims & Legal Frameworks
  • Construction Methods, Equipment & Safety
  • Sustainability and Smart Infrastructure
  • Real Estate Economics & Valuation
  • Emerging Tech: AI, Robotics, Automation
  • Research Methodology & Consultancy Practice

FAQs

  • What is the duration of the programme?

    The MBA Construction Management is a two-year full-time postgraduate programme divided into four semesters.

  • Who can apply for this programme?

    Graduates from Civil Engineering, Architecture, Planning, Construction Engineering, Infrastructure-related disciplines, or equivalent fields are eligible to apply.

  • What career opportunities are available after completing the programme?

    Graduates can pursue careers as Construction Project Managers, Planning Engineers, BIM Managers, Contract Managers, Project Consultants, Infrastructure Development Managers, and Real Estate Project Managers.

  • Does the program provide industry exposure?

    Yes, the programme includes industry internships, site visits, live projects, and interactions with industry experts.

  • What skills will students develop in this programme?

    Students will develop expertise in construction project planning, cost and contract management, risk management, digital construction technologies, leadership, and strategic decision-making.

  • Which industries recruit graduates of this programme?

    Graduates can work in construction companies, EPC contractors, infrastructure developers, real estate firms, project management consultants, and government infrastructure agencies
