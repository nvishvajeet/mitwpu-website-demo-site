Programme Overview

The B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Mechanical Engineering course integrates school education with a professional career in engineering, making it a balanced, directed programme suited for students after class 10. This allows them to smoothly transition into a complete mechanical engineering career. This six-year programme encompasses fundamental subjects such as mathematics and science before progressing to advanced aspects of mechanical engineering, including machinery design, manufacturing, and the Internet of Things, AI, and automation.

The programme aims to combine theoretical knowledge with practical application. It equips you for adaptable engineering positions in sectors such as automotive, construction, and robotics. MIT-WPU's degree programme, with a strong focus on applied projects, internships, and research, is one of the top mechanical engineering courses in India.

Eligibility

Minimum 60% aggregate marks in the Class 10/ SSC examination from a government-approved board or its equivalent, with Science and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. (Minimum 55% aggregate marks for candidates belonging to the Reserved Category from Maharashtra State).

Selection Process

Admission will be based on the MIT-WPU CET 2026 Entrance Examination and the Personal Interaction (PI) score, conducted by the University as per the prescribed schedule.

Programme Highlights

  • Integrative Curriculum : It combines essential learning with specialised mechanical engineering principles, preparing students to take on advanced technical responsibilities.

FAQs

  • Are there scholarships available for this programme?

    Yes, MIT-WPU offers 2000+ merit-based scholarships and financial assistance to help students excel academically and professionally. For more detailed information visit our website: https://mitwpu.edu.in/scholarships

  • Which are the main subjects in the programme?

    The programme covers topics related to mechanical engineering and robotics, including mechanical design, control engineering, robotics, AI, IoT, and automation. Through a combination of theory and practical applications, students learn to build systems incorporating robotics, AI, automation models, and data-driven decision-making.

  • What types of experiential learning opportunities are available?

    MIT-WPU offers industry internships, immersion programmes and hands-on projects in sectors such as automotive, manufacturing, and robotics. Students also get the opportunity to work on live projects across AI, IoT, and robotics, gaining hands-on experience that prepares them for their future careers.

  • Is there any kind of placement assistance at MIT-WPU?

    Through the Center of Industry-Academia Partnerships, the university offers 100% placement assistance, connecting students with top-tier firms. Career guidance, resume building workshops, mock interviews ensure that students are industry-ready.

  • Why choose the B.Tech. After 10th (3 years Diploma + 3 years Degree) in Mechanical Engineering with Robotics and Automation?

    This combined Mechanical Engineering and Robotics & Automation programme equips students with expertise in machine and robot design. It covers foundational engineering principles as well as advanced topics such as AI, robotics, and IoT, preparing students for the tech jobs of the future.
